# 1. Gabatarwa

Barka da zuwa tsarin *Basic Roleplaying* na Chaosium, ɗaya daga cikin tsarin wasannin kwaikwayo na matsayi (roleplaying games) mafi tasiri a duniya.

Idan ka saba da wasannin kwaikwayo, za ka iya tsallake yawancin wannan gabatarwar. Wannan sashe yana bayani ne kan kalmomin da ake amfani da su a cikin tsarin *Basic Roleplaying*. Ga tsofaffin ’yan wasa, sashe ɗaya tilo da ya kamata ka kula da shi a cikin wannan gabatarwar shi ne sashen Dokoki na Zaɓi (Optional Rules).

## Kalmomin da ake amfani da su a Basic Roleplaying

Ana amfani da kalmomin nan a kai a kai a cikin littafin nan. Wasu daga cikinsu sanannu ne a duniyar wasannin kwaikwayo, kuma an kawo su ne don taimaka wa sababbi da ƙwararrun ’yan wasa. Kowane kalma an yi mata bayani dalla-dalla a sassan da suka dace.

**Ikon Iyawa (Ability):** Wani abu da ɗan wasa zai iya yi ko ji, ko dai fasaha (skill) ce, sha’awa (passion), ko wani abin da aka ba shi daraja tsakanin 1–100. Gabaɗaya, idan ka yi nasarar amfani da wata iyawa, za ka samu "tabbacin gogewa" (experience check).

**Aminci (Allegiance):** Wani tsarin zaɓi da ke auna biyayya ga wani abu na ruhaniya, ƙa’ida, ko abin bauta, wanda ake ƙara ƙarfinsa ta hanyar yin ayyukan da aka fi so.

**Ƙarin Ƙarfi (Augment):** Amfani da wata iyawa don gyara damar samun nasara ta amfani da wata iyawar, kamar lokacin da wata fasaha ta ba da ƙarin tallafi ga wata, ko kuma wata sha’awa za ta iya taimakawa wajen inganta damar samun nasarar wata fasahar.

**Damar Farko (Base Chance):** Damar da aka saba da ita na samun nasara da fasahar da ɗan wasa bai taɓa samun horo ko gogewa a kai ba.

***Misali,*** *a takardar ɗan wasa, "Hide (20%)" na nufin koda ɗan wasanka bai saka maki a fasahar ɓuya (Hide) ba, yana da damar 20% ta ɗabi’a a cikinta.*

**Nisan Harbi (Base Range):** Matsakaicin nisan da makamin harbi zai iya buga hari.

**Ɗan Wasa (Character):** Matsayin da kake ɗauka a cikin zaman wasa, wanda aka bayyana shi a cikin tsarin wasan ta hanyar ƙimomi kamar halaye (characteristics) da jimlar fasaha. Gabaɗaya, ’yan wasa suna da suna da tarihin rayuwa da ɗan wasan ya yanke, tare da taimako da/ko amincewar jagoran wasa (gamemaster). (duba kuma **ɗan wasan mutum** da **ɗan wasan da ba mutum ba**).

**Halaye (Characteristic):** Halayen jiki, tunani, da ruhaniya na ɗan wasanka (Ƙarfi/Strength, Ƙarfin Jiki/Constitution, Girma/Size, Hankali/Intelligence, Iko/Power, Ɗabi’a/Dexterity, da Kwarjini/Charisma), waɗanda aka ƙididdige su a kan ma’auni (yawanci 3–18), inda matsakaicin mutum yake 10-11.

***Misali,*** *idan matsakaicin farko na wani hali shi ne 18, STR 17 na nufin ɗan wasanka yana da ƙarfi sosai, yayin da DEX 7 na nufin ɗan wasan yana da ɗan saurin tuntube (clumsy).*

**Jajircewar Hali (Characteristic Roll):** Ɗaya daga cikin halayen ɗan wasanka wanda aka ninka da wani lamba (Hankali ×3, misali), wanda aka bayyana a matsayin kashi, kuma aka yi jifa da zaruman kashi (percentile dice). Jajircewar hali yawanci ×5 ne, amma ana iya amfani da wasu ninnikuwa.

***Misali,*** *idan hankalin ɗan wasanka 14 ne, yana da damar 42% na samun nasara tare da jajircewar hali mai ninka ×3.*

**Zagayen Yaƙi (Combat Round):** Lokaci na daƙiƙa 12 (ko makamancin haka) da aka ɗora yayin yaƙi ko ayyukan ban sha’awa, inda tsarin faruwar ayyuka yake da muhimmanci. Wannan lokacin wasa ne, ba lokacin gaske ba—abubuwan da suka faru a cikin ƴan daƙiƙoƙi na iya ɗaukar mintuna da yawa tsakanin ’yan wasa da jagoran wasa.

**Babban Nasara (Critical Success):** Wannan shi ne sakamakon jifan fasaha wanda ya kai 1/20 (ko 5%) na damar da aka saba da ita ta samun nasara. Gabaɗaya, babban nasara yana samun lada mafi girma, kodayake a wasu lokuta babban nasara yana zama dole don samun nasara kwata-kwata.

***Misali,*** *idan damar samun nasara ta yau da kullun ita ce 75%, duk wani jifa na 4 ko ƙasa da haka babban nasara ne (1/20 na 75% shi ne 3.75, an zagaya zuwa 4).*

**D100:** Jifan zaruman kashi (percentile die), wanda ake samu ta hanyar jefa zaruman kashi guda biyu masu gefe 10 (D10s), ɗaya na wakiltar goman, ɗaya kuma na wakiltar ɗayan. Wasu zaruma sun riga sun zo da alamun ‘goma’ (00, 10, 20, da dai sauransu) kuma ana amfani da su koyaushe azaman lamba ta farko a jifan D100. Jifan 01 shi ne sakamako mafi kyau, yayin da 100 (wanda yawanci ake karantawa a matsayin 00) shi ne mafi muni.

***Misali,*** *jifan 60 (ko 6) da 2 ya zama sakamakon 62.*

**Lahani (Damage):** Ƙimar da ke wakiltar rauni, wanda ake cirewa daga maki na lafiya (hit points) na ɗan wasa. Makamai, abubuwan da suka faru ba zato ba tsammani, da sauran haɗari suna haifar da lahani ga ƴan wasa.

**Gyaran Lahani (Damage Modifier):** Gyaran da ake yi wa lahani saboda halayen Ƙarfi (Strength) da Girma (Size) da suka wuce matsakaici.

**DEX Rank:** Dangane da halin Ɗabi’a (Dexterity), wannan yana ƙayyade lokacin da ɗan wasanka zai iya yin aiki yayin zagayen yaƙi. ƴan wasa masu DEX mafi girma yawanci za su yi aiki kafin ƴan wasa masu DEX mafi ƙanƙanta.

**Gyaran Wahala (Difficulty Modifier):** Adadin da ake gyara damar fasaha, dangane da yanayin da ke kewaye da amfani da ita. Waɗannan sun kama daga Ta atomatik (ba buƙatar jifa), Mai sauƙi (an ninka fasaha sau biyu), Matsakaici (babu gyara), da Wahala (an raba fasaha gida biyu) har zuwa Ba zai yiwu ba (babu jifa, ko damar 01% bisa ga shawarar jagoran wasa).

**Jifan Gogewa (Experience Rolls):** Idan ka yi nasara da jifa don ɗaya ko fiye na iyawar ɗan wasanka ko jajircewar halaye (duba ƙasa), ya kamata ka yi alama a kan takardar ɗan wasanka a cikin akwatin da aka tanada (wani lokacin ana kiran wannan aikin da "tabbacin gogewa"), idan ya dace. Daga baya, tsakanin abubuwan ban sha’awa ko yayin lokacin hutu, za ka iya tantance ko iyawar ɗan wasanka ta inganta a waccan fasahar ko kuma halin ya ƙaru ta hanyar amfani da nasara. Wasu fasahohin ba za a iya inganta su da gogewa ba kuma dole ne a inganta su ta wasu hanyoyi.

**Gazawa (Fail):** Jifan zaruman kashi sama da damar da ake buƙata don samun nasara.

***Misali,*** *jifan 89 lokacin da damar da ake buƙata ita ce 56% yana nuna gazawa.*

**Raunin Mutuwa (Fatal Wound):** Raunin da ke sa ɗan wasanka rasa maki na lafiya fiye da abin da yake da shi a halin yanzu. Ba tare da taimako ba (na likita ko akasin haka), mutuwa tana zuwa ne a ƙarshen zagayen yaƙi na gaba.

**Makin Gajiyawa (Fatigue Points):** Wani tsarin zaɓi, ta amfani da halayen Ƙarfi (Strength) da Ƙarfin Jiki (Constitution) na ɗan wasanka don tantance tsawon lokacin da zai iya jure ayyukan jiki mai nauyi kafin ya gaji.

**Cikakken Juyi (Full Turn):** Lokacin da ya ƙunshi zagayen yaƙi 25, wanda ya yi daidai da mintuna biyar na lokacin duniyar wasa.

**Tuntube (Fumble):** Jifan 99 da/ko 00 lokacin jifan zaruman kashi don tantance nasara. Gabaɗaya, lokacin da aka nuna tuntube, wani mummunan abu yana faruwa wanda ya wuce gazawa ta yau da kullun.

**Jagoran Wasa (Gamemaster - GM):** Daraktan, ko jagora don wasan, wanda ke taimaka maka ƙirƙirar ƴan wasa, ya fito da abubuwan ban sha’awa, kuma yana wakiltar duniyar da ke wajen ɗan wasanka, yana jefa zaruma don ƴan wasan da ba mutane ba (NPCs) da sojojin adawa.

**Lokacin Wasa (Game Time):** Lokacin da yake wucewa yayin da yanayi ko yaƙin neman zaɓe (campaign) ke bayyana kamar yadda ƴan wasanka suka fuskanta. Wannan yawanci ba iri ɗaya ba ne da lokacin gaske da kai da jagoran wasa kuke kashewa kuna wasa.

**Wurin da Aka Buga (Hit Location):** Wani tsarin zaɓi don tantance inda nasarar harin ya sauka a jikin ɗan wasanka, ko inda hare-harensu suka buga abokin gaba.

**Makin Lafiya (Hit Points):** Ma’aunin lafiyar ɗan wasanka, wanda aka wakilta a cikin ƙimar da aka samo daga halayen Ƙarfin Jiki (Constitution) da Girma (Size). Masu hari suna haifar da lahani a cikin maki na lafiya, wanda aka cire daga jimlar maki na lafiya na manufa. Yawanci lokacin da ɗan wasanka ya kai maki 0 na lafiya, sun mutu.

**"Cikin hali" ko "Wajen hali" ("In character" or "Out of character"):** Bambanci a cikin tattaunawa tsakanin ƴan wasa da jagoran wasa a kusa da teburin wasa, wanda ke wakiltar bambanci tsakanin tattaunawar duniya ta gaske da tattaunawar wasa. Dukansu suna cimma manufa ɗaya, amma suna wakiltar salo ko fannoni daban-daban na wasa, kuma ana iya amfani da su ta hanyar musanya a wasa. Wasu ƙungiyoyi ko ƴan wasa suna karkata zuwa ga ɗaya ko ɗayan—abin da aka fi so ne. Tattaunawar "Cikin hali" tana da daɗi kuma tana ɗaukar hankali, yayin da tattaunawar "wajen hali" take da mahimmanci don bayyana ƙa’idojin wasa da kuma saurin wasa ta hanyar cikakken bayani da ba dole ba.

***Misali,*** *cikin hali, za ka iya cewa ‘Sir Wilfric ya tambayi ma’aikacin otal “Shin za ka iya ba mu izinin zama na dare ɗaya don alƙawarin biya a nan gaba!”’ yayin da wajen hali, za ka iya cewa “Ina ƙoƙarin shawo kan (Persuade) ma’aikacin otal ɗin ya bar mu mu zauna na dare ɗaya ba tare da biya ba.”*

**Makin Sihiri (Magic Points):** Wani suna don maki na iko (duba **power points**).

**Babban Rauni (Major Wound):** Raunin da ke haifar da isasshen lahani don wuce rabin jimlar maki na lafiya na ɗan wasa a bugu ɗaya. Yawanci yana tare da sakamako mai raunana.

**Ƙaramin Rauni (Minor Wound):** Duk wani rauni guda ɗaya wanda bai kai babban rauni ba (ƙasa da 1/2 na maki na lafiya na yau da kullun).

**Masu Gyara (Modifiers):** Ƙari ko raguwa na wucin gadi ga ƙimar ɗan wasanka a cikin iyawa, yawanci daga yanayi, muhalli, ko kayan aiki.

***Misali,*** *ƙoƙarin buɗe ƙyallen ƙofa da takarda mai lankwasa misali ne na kayan aikin da ba su kai matsayi ba, don gyaran –20%. Amfani da maƙullin ƙofa na yau da kullun ba ya gyara fasahar. Amfani da tsarin kayan aikin buɗe ƙofa na musamman yana ƙara +20% ga ƙimar fasahar ɗan wasanka.*

**Motsi (MOV):** Gudun motsi da ɗan wasanka zai iya yi yayin zagayen yaƙi ɗaya. Wannan ma’auni ne mai sassauci, amma yawanci yana nufin mita ɗaya na motsi a kowane maki na MOV.

**Ɗan Wasan da Ba Mutum Ba (Nonplayer Character - NPC):** Ɗan wasa ko halitta a cikin duniyar wasa wanda jagoran wasa yake takawa ko wakilta, maimakon kai ko wani ɗan wasa ya taka shi.

**Jifan Adawa (Opposed Roll):** Lokacin da ɗan wasanka yake son yin ƙoƙarin aikin da wani ɗan wasa (ko ɗan wasan da ba mutum ba) yake son ya nuna adawa da fasahar "akasin haka" wadda take soke ko hana aikin farko. A wannan yanayin, ana yin gwajin fasaha duka biyu, kuma ana kwatanta sakamakon.

***Misali,*** *yawanci ana adawa da Fasahar Ɓuya (Stealth) da Fasahar Saurara (Listen).*

**Sha’awa (Passion):** Wani tsarin zaɓi inda aka auna imanin da aka riƙe sosai ga wani batu—kamar Biyayya, Tsoro, Ƙiyayya, Ƙauna, Aminci—a kan tushen kashi. Ana amfani da sha’awa don nuna ko tantance yadda ɗan wasanka yake ji game da batun da kuma ƙarfinsa, kuma galibi ana amfani da shi don ƙara ƙarfin jifan iyawa. (duba **augments**).

**Zaruman Kashi (Percentile Dice):** Zaruma biyu na D10s waɗanda aka jefa tare, inda ɗaya aka keɓance a matsayin adadin goma, ɗayan kuma na ɗayan. Wasu zaruma an ƙididdige su a cikin rukunin goma, watau, 00, 10, 20, 30, da sauransu. Duba kuma **D100**.

**Ɗan Wasan Mutum (Player Character - PC):** Wannan shi ne ɗan wasanka, yawanci wanda kake ƙirƙira kuma kusan koyaushe kake sarrafawa, wanda ya ƙunshi iyawa da ainihi da kake ɗauka ta hanyar wasan kwaikwayo. Ɗan wasanka shi ne wakilcinka a cikin duniyar wasa kuma yana ba ka damar yin hulɗa da saitin.

**Iko (Power):** Kalma ta gabaɗaya don sihiri, mutasyon, ikon tunani, tsafi, ko ƙarfin ban mamaki da ɗan wasanka zai iya mallaka (duba Babi na Hudu: Iko).

**Makin Iko (Power Points):** Adadin ƙarfin zuciya ko kuzari da ɗan wasanka yake da shi, wanda aka wakilta a matsayin jimla bisa ga halin Iko (Power). Ana kashe waɗannan don amfani da iko na musamman ko iyawa kuma wasu iyawa na iya kwashe su. Lokacin da ɗan wasanka ya kai maki 0 na iko, suna faɗuwa sumamme. Ana iya kiran maki na iko da **maki na sihiri** a cikin nau’ikan da suka dace.

**Sana’a (Profession):** Aikin ɗan wasanka, wanda aka wakilta azaman jerin fasahohin da aka horar da su don amfani (ko kuma sun sami damar koyo).

**Ƙima (Rating):** Damar samun nasara don iyawa, wanda aka ƙididdige shi azaman ƙima daga 1–100 (wani lokacin sama da haka).

***Misali,*** *Demolitions 43% ƙima ce ta 43, ma’ana 43% damar samun nasara lokacin da ake ƙoƙarin fasahar yayin yanayi na yau da kullun. (duba kuma **skill rating**).*

**Jifan Juriya (Resistance Roll):** Lokacin da ɗan wasanka yake son yin ƙoƙarin aikin da wani abu ko ƙarfi da ba ya motsi yake tsayayya da shi, ana adawa da halin da ya dace (misali, Ƙarfi) da wani halin da ya dace (misali, Ƙarfin ƙofar da ta makale). Ana kwatanta lambobi biyun a kan teburin juriya (duba ƙasa) don tantance damar samun nasara, kuma ɗaya ko duka ƴan wasan suna jefa zaruma don ganin wanene ya yi nasara. Halaye daidai suna nufin 50% damar samun nasara. Hakanan ana iya amfani da jifan juriya lokacin da ake gwabza ɗan wasa ɗaya da wani ɗan wasa, kamar wasan dambe na hannu (Ƙarfi vs. Ƙarfi).

**Teburin Juriya (Resistance Table):** Teburin da ke nuna rabon hali da hali. Don amfani da teburin juriya, nemo ƙarfin adawa a kan ginshiƙin tsaye, ƙarfin tsayayya a kan ginshiƙin a kwance, sannan a kwatanta su don nemo yiwuwar samun nasara, wanda aka bayyana azaman damar kashi.

**Jifa (Roll):** Jifa ta amfani da D100 don tantance ko yunƙurin yin fasaha, iyawa, ko iko ya yi nasara. Sakamakon jifan fasaha shi ne **babban nasara**, **nasara ta musamman**, **nasara**, **gazawa**, ko **tuntube**. Ana gabatar da ƙarin hanyoyin tantance ingancin nasara a cikin dokokin zaɓi.

**Hankali (Sanity):** Wani tsarin zaɓi inda ake auna lafiyar tunanin ɗan wasanka ta hanyar ikon su na jure gani mai ban tsoro, abubuwan da suka faru, da wahayi. Yayin da Hankali ya ragu, fahimtar gaskiya tana raguwa. Idan ɗan wasanka ya rasa Hankali da yawa a lokaci ɗaya, za su iya zama mahaukata na wucin gadi ko na dindindin.

**Fasaha (Skill):** Iyawa, horo, fannin ilimi, baiwa, kwarewa, ko wani abu da ɗan wasanka ya san wani abu game da shi (ko yake da baiwa a cikinsa), wanda aka ƙididdige shi azaman matakin fasaha. Ana amfani da fasahohi a cikin jifan fasaha, inda ake jefa zaruman kashi (percentile dice) a kan ƙimar fasahar.

**Ƙimar Fasaha (Skill Rating):** Matakin kwarewa da ɗan wasanka yake da shi da fasaha, wanda aka bayyana azaman lamba daga 00% (babu fasaha ko kaɗan) zuwa 100% ko fiye (ƙwararren masani a duniya).

**Nasara ta Musamman (Special Success):** Jifan 1/5 na ƙimar da ake buƙata don samun nasara yana nuna cewa ɗan wasanka ya yi rawar gani sosai kuma ya sami sakamako mafi girma fiye da nasara ta al’ada. Wannan yana da mahimmanci musamman a yaƙi.

***Misali,*** *ɗan wasa mai 70% a cikin fasaha wanda ya jefa 14 ko ƙasa da haka ya sami nasara ta musamman.*

**Nasara (Success):** Sakamakon jifan zaruman kashi inda ɗan wasanka ya yi aikin yadda ya kamata, kuma ya sami matsakaicin ingancin nasara. Idan jifan ya yi ƙasa sosai, yana iya zama **nasara ta musamman** ko **babban nasara**, kamar yadda aka bayyana a sama. Idan ya wuce ƙimar iyawa, yawanci **gazawa** ce. Idan ya yi yawa sosai, kamar 00, yana iya zama **tuntube**.

***Misali,*** *idan ɗan wasanka yana da 70% a cikin fasaha, kuma ya jefa 70 ko ƙasa da haka, sun yi nasara.*

### Kayan Aikin da ake Bukata don Wasa

A matsayinka na ɗan wasa, ba kwa buƙatar komai face wannan littafin, fensir, wataƙila takarda fanko, da saitin zaruman wasa. Ana samun zaruma a kan layi a cikin wasanni da yawa da shagunan sha’awa, kuma suna zuwa da launuka iri-iri, siffofi, girma, inganci, da farashi. Za ka buƙaci akalla saiti ɗaya cikakke, ɗaya na kowane, amma ƙari yana da amfani. Duba ƙasa don ƙarin bayani kan zaruma da hanyoyin jefa zaruma. Wasu ƙungiyoyin wasa suna amfani da ƙananan siffofi (miniatures) azaman wakilcin ƴan wasa, don haka idan wannan gaskiya ne ga ƙungiyarka, za ka iya son kawo ƙaramar siffa wadda take kama da ɗan wasanka yadda ya kamata. Gabaɗaya, duk da haka, kawai kana buƙatar kawo kuzarin ƙirƙirarka zuwa wasan.

A matsayinka na jagoran wasa, kana buƙatar ɗan ƙari kaɗan fiye da ɗan wasa, amma ba da yawa ba. Ya kamata ka sami kwafin wannan littafin dokoki, da ƙarin takarda fanko don bayanan ka. Kyakkyawan ra’ayi ne a yi kwafin takardar ɗan wasa da aka bayar ko aka samo a kan layi a chaosium.com.

Bugu da ƙari, ya kamata ka shirya abin ban sha’awa ko akalla ra’ayin abin da zai iya faruwa a cikin wasan. Wannan na iya zama ɗaya daga cikin ƙirƙirarka ko abin ban sha’awa da aka shirya. A madadin, za ka iya daidaita abin ban sha’awa daga wani kamfanin wasa don wasu tsarin wasa. Za ka iya shirya wasu kayan aiki dangane da yadda kake son shirya takardu ko takardun tunani. Za ka ƙara koyo game da duk wannan a Babi na Tara: Jagorantar Wasa.

### Zaruma

*Basic Roleplaying* yana amfani da zaruma masu gefe huɗu, shida, takwas, goma, goma sha biyu, da 20 (wanda aka taƙaita azaman D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, da D20). Yawanci ana jefa zaruma, kuma sakamakon ana karantawa yadda aka nuna, kuma idan an jefa zaruma da yawa, ana haɗa su tare. Don haka, 3D6 yana nufin jefa zaruma guda uku masu gefe shida, kuma a haɗa sakamakon.

Misali, jifan 3D6 wanda ya haifar da 1, 4, da 6 zai zama jimlar 11. Hakanan ana iya haɗa zaruma tare a cikin nau’ikan kuɗi daban-daban, kamar 2D6+1D4, ma’ana jefa zaruma guda biyu masu gefe shida da zaruma mai gefe huɗu, kuma a haɗa jimlar su.

Wani lokaci ana gyara sakamakon zaruma ta hanyar ƙarawa ko cire lambobi ga jifan farko, kamar 1D6–1, ma’ana jefa zaruma ɗaya mai gefe shida kuma a cire 1 daga jimlar, ma’ana jifan yana da kewayon 0 zuwa 5.

Ana iya ninka jimlar zaruma, kamar 1D10×10, ma’ana jifan yana da kewayon 10 zuwa 100.

Wani lokaci jifan zaruma ba sa bayyana kamar suna daidaita da lambobin zaruma daidai, kamar D3, wanda shi ne sakamakon jifan D6 da aka raba gida biyu kuma aka zagaya sama. Jifan D2 ko D5 yana amfani da D4 da D10, bi da bi, kuma ana jefa su kuma ana raba su a cikin salo iri ɗaya. Irin waɗannan jifan suna da wuya.

Zaruman kashi (Percentile dice) su ne D10 guda biyu, da aka jefa tare, tare da zaruma ɗaya azaman adadin goma, ɗayan kuma azaman ɗayan.

Misali, jifan D10 guda biyu na iya ba da 7 da 3, ko 73%.

Jifan 01% ana ɗauka a matsayin manufa, yayin da 100% (jifan 0 da 0) yawanci gazawa ne kuma jifan da ba a so. Yana da gama gari a yi amfani da zaruma masu launuka daban-daban don adadin goma da ɗayan, kodayake zaruman kashi yanzu ana samun su cikin sauƙi tare da goma da aka nuna ta 00, 10, 20, 30, da sauransu don dacewa.

Ayyukan jefa zaruma na waya (Dice rolling apps) suma suna kula da duk wannan kuma suna samuwa gabaɗaya.

## Ga zaruma mafi gama gari:

**D2**: Wannan yana ba da sakamakon 1 ko 2. Kuna iya jefa D4 (ko kowane zaruma mai gefe biyu) kuma ku raba shi gida biyu, tare da jefa tsabar kuɗi (heads = 1, tails = 2), ko ɗaukar kowane zaruma kuma ku jefa shi (sakamakon mara nau’i = 1, sakamako mai nau’i = 2).

**D3**: Jefa D6 kuma ku raba shi gida biyu, zagaya sama.

**D4**: Zaruma mai gefe huɗu, yana ba da sakamakon 1 zuwa 4. Galibi ana amfani da shi tare da ƙarin lahani.

**D6**: Zaruma na biyu mafi gama gari, wannan yana ba da sakamakon 1 zuwa 6. Wataƙila ka riga ka san zaruma kamar waɗannan.

**D8**: Zaruma mai gefe takwas, yana ba da sakamakon 1 zuwa 8.

**D10**: Zaruma mai gefe goma, yana ba da sakamakon 1 zuwa 10, tare da fuskar 0 tana wakiltar 10. Galibi ana amfani da shi tare da wani D10 azaman wani ɓangare na jifan zaruman kashi.

**D12**: Zaruma mai gefe 12, yana ba da sakamakon 1 zuwa 12. Ba a amfani da wannan nau’in zaruma sau da yawa.

**D20**: Zaruma mai gefe 20, yana ba da sakamakon 1 zuwa 20. Wannan nau’in zaruma yana da wuya a yi amfani da shi a wajen tsarin wurin buga hari na zaɓi.

**D100**: Zaruma guda biyu masu gefe goma, waɗanda aka jefa tare tare da zaruma ɗaya tana wakiltar ƙimar goma da wani yana wakiltar ƙimar ɗayan. Wannan shi ne jifan zaruma mafi gama gari a BRP. Kodayake yawanci jifan kashi ne, wani lokaci ana jefa waɗannan zaruman don sakamako na asali.

Misali, jagoran wasa na iya tantance cewa kowane memba na ƙungiyar ƴan fashi yana da tsabar kuɗi D100 a hannunsu. Idan aka ci nasara a kan ƴan fashin, jagoran wasa yana jefa zaruma don kowane ɗaya.

Wasu saitin zaruma na musamman har ma sun haɗa da nau’ikan da ba kasafai ba, kamar D2, D3, da dai sauransu amma ba su da mahimmanci.

> ### Dokoki na Zaɓi (Optional Rules)
> 
> A cikin shekaru da yawa, an gabatar da ƙananan tsarin daban-daban a cikin wasannin *Basic Roleplaying* don rufe yanayi ko sharuɗɗa da suka dace da takamaiman wasan, kamar tsarin yaƙi da aka faɗaɗa don wasannin almara, dokoki don hauka da bincike don wasannin ban tsoro, dokoki don amfani da ababen hawa da jiragen sama, dokoki da ke tafiyar da sha’awa mai girma da halayen ɗabi’a, tsarin sihiri daban-daban da suka dace da nau’in, da sauransu. Har ma an canza mahimman abubuwa ko an bi da su daban, wani lokacin ma a cikin bugu daban-daban na wasa guda.
> 
> Waɗannan bambance-bambancen suna bayyana a nan azaman dokoki na zaɓi, waɗanda aka gabatar a cikin akwatuna kamar wannan, tare da shawarwari kan lokacin da za a yi amfani da su, da bayanin kula game da yadda suke shafar wasa, kuma a wasu lokuta, game da yadda suke hulɗa da wasu dokoki na zaɓi. Don haka, jagoran wasa zai iya zaɓa da kuma zaɓar dokoki na zaɓi kamar yadda ake so.
> 
> Kafin ka fara wasa, ya kamata ka yanke shawarar wane irin dokoki na zaɓi kake son amfani da su, musamman waɗanda suka shafi ƙirƙirar ɗan wasa. *Babi na Goma: Saiti (Settings)* yana ba da shawarar waɗanne dokoki na zaɓi suka dace da takamaiman saiti, kuma ƙari ga haka, an gabatar da cikakken jerin a cikin *Babi na Tara: Jagorantar Wasa*.
