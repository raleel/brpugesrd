# 10. Yanayin Wasan (Settings)

Wannan babi yana magana ne akan yanayin wasan—lokuta da/ko wuraren da kasada kuke ciki suke faruwa. Galibi, yanayin wasan yana wakiltar nau'in labari ne—Western, science fiction, techno-thriller, sword-and-sandal, high fantasy, ko wasu da dama. Wannan babi ya kuma ƙunshi dokoki na zaɓi don aminci (allegiance), sha'awa (passions), da hauka (insanity).

## Ƙirƙirar Yanayin Wasa

*Basic Roleplaying* an tsara shi ne don ya taimake ka, a matsayinka na shugaban wasa (gamemaster), ka ƙirƙiri naka yanayin wasan, ko na asali ne ko kuma wanda aka ɗauko daga wani abu da yake akwai. Ga wasu mashahuran yanayin wasa waɗanda duka suke bin tsari ɗaya, tare da wasu bayanai akan kowannensu. Kowanne yana zuwa da shawarar nau'in halayen ƴan wasa (duk da cewa waɗannan ba ƙa'idodi ba ne), ƙarfin da yake da ma'ana a cikin yanayin wasan, fasahar da za ta taimaka wajen zaɓar kayan aiki, abubuwan da za su jawo nau'in kasada, da kuma dokokin zaɓi da suka dace.

### Zamanin Prehistoric (Kafin Tarihi)

Duk wani yanayin zamani na farko ko na ƙabilu kafin wayewar kai, ko kuma wanda aka ware daga gare shi. Rayuwa gajeriya ce kuma mai tsanani, kuma galibi ƙungiyar zamantakewa ɗaya tilo ita ce dangi ko ƙabila. Mammals na zamanin da galibi su ne babban barazana, duk da cewa wasu tarihi daban-daban wani lokaci suna haɗa da dinosaurs.

* **Nau'in Halaye:** Maƙera (Crafter), Manomi (Farmer), Mafarauci (Hunter), Boka (Shaman), da Mutanen Ƙabila (Tribesfolk).
* **Ƙarfi:** Bokaye na zamanin da za su iya amfani da sihiri, kuma maye gurbi (mutations) da ƙarfin tunani (psychic abilities) na iya wanzuwa.
* **Fasaha:** Babu kayan masana'antu a cikin duniyar zamanin da, duk abin da ake da shi ana siffanta shi ne daga albarkatu na asali kamar itace, dutse, ƙashi, fata, gashi, da yumbu—ko tufafi ne, kayan aiki da makamai, da gumaka na addini ko abubuwan tunawa. Duk da cewa ƙwararren maƙeri zai iya yin gatari na dutse mafi kyau da kuma siffanta kayan kariya mafi kyau, babu abin da ya wuce nan. Yawancin kayan suna da ingancin da ba su da kyau ko na matsakaici, kuma babu dukiya.
* **Kasada:** Rikicin ƙabilu tsakanin ƙungiyoyin masu farauta da tara abinci akan wuraren farauta, yanayi mara kyau, dabbobi masu zafin rai, ko ma manyan farauta na iya zama ginshiƙin kasada na zamanin da. Tarihi daban-daban na iya haɗa da tsoffin halittu daga Cthulhu Mythos, baƙi daga sararin samaniya da ke son bautar da ɗan Adam na farko, ko ma hanyoyin juyin halitta inda ƙwararrun dinosaurs ko birai suka zama jinsin da suka fi kowa ƙarfi, kuma ɗan Adam ya zama abincinsu.
* **Zaɓi:** Babu.

### Zamanin Bronze (Bronze Age)

Zamanin masarautu kamar Sumeria, Girka, Roma, Masar, Farisa, da sauran manyan dauloli, daga zamanin Mesopotamia zuwa zamanin tsohon littafi (Biblical antiquity), Girka na gargajiya, har zuwa faɗuwar Roma da kuma rugujewar Turai. A madadin haka, wannan na iya zama yanayin fantasy na "takobi-da-takalmi" (sword-and-sandal) da aka saita a cikin wata duniyar da aka yi tunaninta.

* **Nau'in Halaye:** Kowane nau'i ban da Ɗan Jarida (Journalist), Masanin Kimiyya (Scientist), da Mai Fasaha (Technician).
* **Ƙarfi:** Sihiri da bokanci, wanda wataƙila asalin sa na allahntaka ne. Ƙarfin tunani (psychic powers) na iya wanzuwa, kuma dodanni na iya samun maye gurbi. Rabin-alloli ko jarumai na tatsuniya na iya samun ƙarfi na musamman (superpowers).
* **Fasaha:** Zamanin girman Zamanin Bronze da kuma furen Zamanin Iron, inda aikin ƙarfe ya canza al'adu da kasuwanci. Wannan shi ne lokacin bunƙasar fasaha da haihuwar wayewar kai.
* **Kasada:** Wannan zamanin ya dace da labaran fantasy masu tsauri da kuma jigogi na tatsuniya da aka jaddada akan al'ada da bauta.
* **Zaɓi:** Aminci (Allegiance), Hare-hare da Kariya (Attacks and Parries) sama da 100%, Zaɓar Ƙimar Halaye (Choosing Characteristic Values), Gyaran Al'adu (Cultural Modifiers), Lalacewa da Wuraren da aka buge (Damage and Hit Locations), Guje wa Makamai (Dodging Missile Weapons), Maki na Gajiya (Fatigue Points), Ƙimar Halaye ta Farko Mafi Girma (Higher Starting Characteristics), Maki na Lafiya (Hit Points) a kowane wuri, Ƙarin Maki na Fasaha na Keɓaɓɓu (Increased Personal Skill Points), Ilimin Karatu (Literacy), Sha'awa (Passions), Amfani da Ƙarfi (Power Use) a Matakin Aiki (Action Phase), Suna (Reputation), Ƙimar Fasaha (Skill Ratings) sama da 100%, Jimlar Maki na Lafiya (Total Hit Points).
