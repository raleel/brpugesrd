## Waikawa (Acid)

Ko te pānga ki te waikawa, ki ngā matū pirau rānei, he mōrearea. Me whakaaro he rite ngā waikawa katoa: ko te taumata o te kino o te waikawa e tohu ana i te waimehatanga, kaua ko te momo waikawa motuhake. Ko te whakapā nui (te rumaki o te peka, te whakapā rānei ki ngā kanohi) anake e tika ana kia puta ai he kino nui tonu.

* Ko ngā waikawa ngoikore e tukuna ana te 1D3–1 ngā tohu kino ia rauna.

* Ko ngā waikawa kaha e tukuna ana te 1D4 ngā tohu kino ia rauna.

* Ko ngā waikawa tino kaha e tukuna ana te 1D6+1 ngā tohu kino ia rauna.

Kāore te pūrua e whakakore i te waikawa engari ka whakaroa i ōna pānga. Mena he pūrua tō te whāinga, me tapahi i te kino ki te haurua (me whakamborotahi ki runga), ahakoa me whakarerekē e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu i runga i te momo, te kounga, me te uhi o te pūrua. Ko te whakamarumaru o te pūrua ki ngā waikawa e mau ana mō te 1D3 rauna. Whai muri i tērā, ka tukuna e te waikawa tana kino tūturu.

Ko te pānga ki te waikawa tino kaha e horo ana i ngā here o te pūrua me te whakangoikore i te pūrua: whai muri i te 1D6 rauna o te whakapā, kua kore te pūrua e whai hua, he para noa. Ka puta tēnei i te wā kotahi me ngā rauna 1D3 o te whakamarumaru o te pūrua—kua kore pea te pūrua e whai hua i te whawhai engari kei te tuku whakamarumaru taupua tonu i te waikawa.

Ka whakatau pea tō kaiwhakahaere kēmu he rite te mahi a te wai tapu (he mea rite rānei) ki te waikawa ki ngā mea ngaro, pēnei i ngā waipiro, i ngā hinonga tēmoni rānei.

## Whawhai Rererangi

Kei te pūkenga Rere (Fly) ngā kōrero mō te rere. Mena kāore tō pūāhua e hiahia ana kia whakamahia ōna ringa e rua mō te rere me/rānei te whakatikatika i ngā mihini (hei tauira, ngā mana pēke-jet), ka taea e ia te whakaeke i a ia e rere ana. Ka rite ki te eke hōiho, ka herea te whakaeke me ētahi atu pūkenga whawhai a tō pūāhua e tōna pūkenga Rere: ka whakahekea ngā whakatauranga pūkenga whawhai ki te taumata o te pūkenga Rere a tō pūāhua; mehemea he iti ake, kāore te pūkenga e pā.

Mena ka taea e tō pūāhua te hanga i te takahuri Rere angitu i te wā o te wāhanga mana o te rauna whawhai (kāore tēnei e utu i te mahi), karekau he here ki ngā whakaeke, ngā parai, me ētahi atu mahi whawhai. Ka pā pea ngā whakarerekē e whai ake nei ki te kaha o tō pūāhua ki te whakamahi i te pūkenga Rere. Tirohia hoki ngā **Tikanga Huarere** mō te ua, te kōhatu, te ua hukapapa, me te huka.

* **Takahuri Māmā (pēnei i te huri-pārera):** Kāore he whiu.

* **Takahuri Waenga (takahuri):** –10% te whiu.

* **Takahuri Uaua (wingover):** –25% te whiu.

* **Takahuri Tino Uaua (loop-the-loop):** –50% te whiu.

* **Matangi Māmā, Waenga rānei:** kāore he whiu.

* **Matangi Kaha:** –10% te whiu (kāore he whiu mō te matangi kaha mai i muri).

* **Matangi Tino Kaha:** –25% te whiu (–10% te whiu mō te matangi tino kaha mai i muri).

* **Āwhā:** –50% te whiu (–25% mō te matangi mai i muri).

* **Matangi Ruarīra:** –75% te whiu (–50% te whiu mō te matangi mai i muri).

* **Matangi Tāwhiri:** –100% te whiu.

* **Teitei Rawa / Pēhanga Iti / Ngāwari:** –25% te whiu.

* **Mau Pūrua Māmā:** –10% te whiu.

* **Mau Pūrua Waenga:** –25% te whiu.

* **Mau Pūrua Kaha:** –50% te whiu.

Ko ngā whakarerekē katoa he tupu tonu.

Ki te rahua te takahuri Rere, ko ngā mahi a te pūāhua e rere ana he *Uaua*. Ko te angitu whakahirahira mō te takahuri Rere e hanga ana i ngā takahuri i mahia i taua rauna whawhai kia *Māmā*. Ki te hinga te takahuri Rere, kāore e taea e tō pūāhua te whakaeke, te mahi rānei i taua rauna, ā, ka hinga pea, ka tukituki rānei.

Ki te whiwhi i te rererangi mā te waka, te mea rere rānei, kāore tō pūāhua e hiahia ki te hanga i ngā takahuri Rere—ko te pūkenga e hiahiatia ana ko te Eke, ko te Pairati rānei, ko te pūkenga Rere rānei o te mea e rere ana, mehemea e hāngai ana. Ahakoa te aha, ka pā ngā pānga o te otinga o te takahuri pūkenga ki te kaieke me te mea e rere ana.

## Whakaū i te Whāinga

E rua ngā huarahi e taea ai e tō pūāhua te whakapai ake i tōna tupono ki te mahi i te pupuhi motuhake: te whakapau i te wā me te whakaū, te whāinga rānei i tētahi rohe motuhake. Ka taea te whakamahi i ngā mea e rua mō te pupuhi kotahi. Mena ka whakararuraru tō pūāhua (whakaekea, panaia, me ētahi atu) i te wā e whakamahi ana i tētahi o ēnei tikanga, me hanga e ia he takahuri Whakaaro angitu kia mau tonu te whāinga. Mena ka rahua te takahuri, ka ngaro ngā painga.

### Te Whakapau i te Wā

Ka taea e tō pūāhua te whakapau wā ki te whakaū i tētahi pū pere hei utu mō te whakatauranga pūkenga kua piki. Mō ia rima ngā taumata DEX e whakaroa ai tō pūāhua, tāpirihia te 1/10 o te whakatauranga pūkenga o nāianei (me whakamborotahi ki runga) hei pāpiringa, he tupu tonu, mō te pupuhi tuatahi. Me whakamborotahi ngā hautau ki runga. Me mahi te whakaeke pere i te rauna whawhai kotahi mena he pū a-ringa (kopere, maripi maka, me ētahi atu), ka taea rānei te pupuri mō te wā mutunga kore mena he pū a-whaiaro (pū, pū pūngao, me ētahi atu). Kia mahia te pupuhi, kāore e pā te pāpiringa. Karekau he pānga o te whakaū ātaahua ki ngā awhe i tua atu i te rua o te awhe tūturu o te pū. Mena e whakaū ana ki tētahi tatau, ki tētahi wāhi iti rānei e tatari ana kia puta he whāinga, me haurua, iti iho rānei te whāinga o te awhe tūturu.

### Te Whāinga i tētahi Rohe Motuhake

Ko te whāinga i tētahi wāhanga motuhake o te tinana o tētahi patunga he *Uaua*, ahakoa ko te whakaroa mō ngā taumata 5 DEX (pēnei i te Whakapau Wā, ki runga) ka taea te utu i tēnei whakarerekē. Hoatu te whakarerekē Uaua i mua i ngā whakarerekē mō te whakapau i te wā. Me whakatau te kaiwhakahaere kēmu i te otinga o ngā whakaeke angitu ki ngā rohe kua whāia, pēnei i te takahuri āhuatanga Uaua kia mau tonu te pū, kia tū tonu, me ētahi atu. Ka whai hua pea te pūnaha wāhi pā. Mena ka whāia e tō pūāhua tētahi rohe kore-pūrua, ā, ka angitu, ka warewarehia te pūrua o te whāinga, me he mea i takahurihia he pā whakahirahira.

## Pāmamao (Ambushes)

I te wā o te whakaeke ohorere, ka whiwhi ngā kaiwhakaeke i te painga o ngā whakaeke e kore e taea te whakahoki. Kia angitu ai te pāmamao, me hanga e te kaiwhakaeke he takahuri pūkenga Titiro (Stealth) angitu, e whakahēhia ana e te pūkenga Whakarongo, Rongo, Titiro rānei a te whāinga e hāngai ana, ko tēhea e tika ana.

* Mena kei te whakamahi ngā kaiwhakaeke i ngā pū pere, ā, kāore i kitea, ka whiwhi ngā kaiwhakaeke i te rauna koreutu o ngā whakaeke *Māmā*. Kāore e taea e ngā whāinga te karo, te parai rānei i tēnei rauna tuatahi o ngā whakaeke.
* Mena kei te whakamahi ngā kaiwhakaeke i ngā pū pere, ā, i kitea, ka whiwhi ngā kaiwhakaeke i te rauna koreutu o ngā whakaeke *Māmā*, engari ka taea e ngā whāinga te karo, te parai rānei i tēnei rauna tuatahi o ngā whakaeke.
* Mena kei te whakamahi ngā kaiwhakaeke i ngā pū ā-ringa, ā, kāore anō i hanga i ngā takahuri Titiro angitu, me hanga e ngā whāinga he takahuri Whakarongo, Rongo, Titiro rānei *Māmā* angitu kia kite i ngā kaiwhakaeke e haere mai ana. Mena ka rahua e ngā whāinga te kite i ngā kaiwhakaeke, ko ngā whakaeke ki a rātou he *Māmā*, ā, ko ngā parai, ngā karo rānei e mahia ana e rātou he *Uaua*.
* Mena kei te whakamahi ngā kaiwhakaeke i ngā pū ā-ringa, ā, kei te mōhio te whāinga ki a rātou me te takahuri Whakarongo, Rongo, Titiro rānei angitu, kāore he whakarerekē o ngā whakatauranga pūkenga a ngā kaiwhakaeke, ā, ka taea e ngā whāinga te parai, te Karo rānei i te tikanga tūturu engari kāore e taea te whakahoki, te neke rānei tae noa ki te rauna whawhai e whai ake nei.

I te nuinga o ngā wā, ka tiakina e te pūrua o te whāinga i te tikanga tūturu, hāunga mehemea kei te whakamahi ngā kaiwhakaeke i ngā whakaeke whakaū (tirohia ki runga) kia karo i te pūrua.

I muri i te rauna tuatahi o te ohorere, ka huri te pāmamao hei rauna whawhai tūturu mō ngā taha katoa e whai wāhi ana, me te kore o ngā whakarerekē nā te ohorere.

## Whakaeke Rohe

I te wā e whakaekea ana tētahi rohe me te hiahia ki te tuku kino ki te tangata i roto i taua rohe, mai i te riri o te atua ki ngā poma ā-ringa, ka taea e ngā pūāhua i taua rohe te whakamātau i ngā takahuri Karo kia karo katoa i te kino, i te takahuri Kaha rānei kia haurua te kino (whiringa a te kaitākaro) mā te haere ki muri i te uhi. Ahakoa te aha, e whakaarohia ana ko te hunga i roto i te rohe i mua kua maka i a rātou ki te takoto kia kore ai e whara (tirohia te **Takoto**). Ko te whakatika mai i te takoto e 5 ngā taumata DEX. Mena kāore te whakaeke rohe i karohia, ka pāngia aunoatia e ia te katoa i roto i tōna rohe pānga. I runga i te momo whakaeke, ka puta pea tēnei i te hinga, i te tahuna rānei (tirohia te **Mātao me te Wera**). I te whakaaro o te kaiwhakahaere kēmu, ka taea e te whakaruruhau, te uhi rānei te whakaiti i tēnei kino. Me mahi te pūrua ki te kino mai i ngā whakaeke rohe.

## Pūrua kua Pakaru

Ko te waikawa, te ahi, me ētahi atu whakaeke pirau, kaha rānei ka taea te pakaru, te whakangaro rānei i te pūrua. Ka taea hoki te whakangaro i tētahi wāhanga pūrua e ngā pā whakahirahira, e ngā hinga rānei, ahakoa kei te kaiwhakahaere kēmu tēnei whakatau. Mena kua pakaru te pūrua, kua ngaro rānei ētahi wāhanga, whakaititia tōna uara pūrua ki te 1/2, me whakamborotahi ki raro.

***Hei tauira,*** *ko te pūrua e 5 ngā tohu whakamarumaru ka heke ki te 2 anake ngā tohu uara pūrua. Mena ka whakamahia te uara pūrua matapōkere, ko te pūrua e 1D8+1 ka heke pea ki te 1D4 whakamarumaru, koia nei te 1/2 o 1D8, me te +1 kua whakamborotahia ki raro.*

Ko te pūrua kua pakaru kē, ā, kua pakaru anō i mua i te whakatikatika, ka whakangarotia (ka ngaro katoa te uara whakamarumaru, engari ka mau tonu ngā whiu me te ENC).

Ko te whakatikatika i te pūrua he utu haurua anō i te utu tūturu o te pūrua. Ka taea tēnei te whakarerekē i runga i ngā āhuatanga, pēnei i te whanaungatanga o tō pūāhua ki te kaimahi, te wātea o ngā rawa e hiahiatia ana mō te whakatikatika, me te āhua o te kino. Ko te pūrua kua tino whakangarotia ka taea te whakatikatika i te utu katoa o te pūrua, engari ka 1 tohu iti iho tōna uara pūrua.

## Whakaeke Aura

Ka taea e tō pūāhua te whakamātau i te whakaeke aura, e tono ana i te aro me te whakaatu i te aroaro nui me te kaha o te hiahia. Ka taea hoki te whakamahi i ngā whakaeke aura hei rauemi tino nui i roto i ngā kēmu epika, kēmu o ngā toa nui rānei. Kia mahi i te whakaeke aura, me ine e tō pūāhua te toharite o tōna SIZ, POW, me te CHA (me whakamborotahi ki runga) ki te uara e kīia nei ko te tapeke aura. Ko ngā whakaeke aura me whai rauna whawhai katoa hei tīmata mena kei te whawhai, me whai rānei i te 12 hēkona o te whakaū mena kāore i te whawhai. Ka puta te whakaeke aura i te wāhanga mana o te rauna whawhai.

I te wā e mahia ana te whakaeke aura, me hanga e te tangata e kite ana i tō pūāhua he takahuri ātete me tōna āhuatanga POW ki te tapeke aura o tō pūāhua. Ka whakarerekē pea ētahi, te katoa rānei o ngā āhuatanga e whai ake nei i te takahuri ātete:

* **Tono:** Ka taea e tō pūāhua te whakamātau i te takahuri Tono i mua i te mahi i te whakaeke aura. Mena ka angitu, tāpirihia te +1 ki te tapeke aura, he angitu motuhake +2, me te angitu whakahirahira +3. Ko te takahuri hinga e whakaiti ana i te tapeke aura ki te haurua (me whakamborotahi ki runga).

* **Angitu o Mua:** Mena kua angitu tō pūāhua i te whakaeke aura i mua ki tētahi whāinga, tāpirihia te +1 ki te tapeke aura. Ka taea e te pūāhua waimarie, kaha rānei te mahi i ngā whakaeke aura tonu ki tētahi hoariri kia noho mataku ai rātou, kia whakamomori rānei i a rātou i te mataku.

* **Wheako:** I te whakaaro o te kaiwhakahaere kēmu, ko te ingoa rongonui, te hītori o mua me te whāinga, he takahuri Ingoa angitu rānei ka taea te tāpiri +1–3 ki te tapeke aura.

* **Ngākau Nui:** Ko te takahuri Ngākau Nui angitu e hāngai ana ka taea te tāpiri +1 ki te tapeke aura, he angitu motuhake +2, me te angitu whakahirahira +3. Ko te takahuri hinga e whakaiti ana i te tapeke aura ki te haurua (me whakamborotahi ki runga).

* **Āhuatanga:** Ka taea hoki e te āhuatanga te whakarerekē i te tapeke aura o tō pūāhua ki te pai, te POW rānei o te whāinga ki te kino mā te ±1–3. Ko ngā take pea ko te whakaatu i te taonga makutu kaha me ngā kounga pakiwaitara, te iti rawa, te nui rawa rānei o te pū, te mate o ngā hoariri whawhai, te hinga o mua, te ahua whara rānei, tētahi riri rānei e hono ana ki te whakaeke aura.

Mena ka rahua te takahuri ātete, ka noho mesmerized te (ngā) whāinga o te whakaeke aura mō te toenga o te rauna whawhai. Ka taea e rātou te karo, te parai, te whakaeke rānei ki a rātou, engari kāore e taea te tīmata i tētahi whakaeke, i ētahi atu mahi rānei i runga i tō rātou hiahia.

Ka mutu tēnei mesmerization i te mutunga o te rauna whawhai. Mena he hinga te takahuri ātete, ka noho demoralized te whāinga, ka tuku pea, ka rere rānei, e tika ana. Ko ngā mahi katoa ka noho *Uaua* mō te whāinga demoralized mō te toenga o taua rauna whawhai. Ahakoa he rahua, he hinga rānei te otinga, ka mutu ngā otinga katoa i te taumata DEX whakamutunga o te rauna whawhai i mahia ai te whakaeke aura.

Ko ngā whakaeke aura kāore e tukuna te whakamataku, kāore hoki e pā ki te ngaronga o te hinengaro.

## Pupuhitahi (Autofire)

He maha ngā pū, pēnei i ngā pū mīhini me ngā pū mīhini iti, ka taea te pupuhi i ngā pupuhi poto (ngā pupuhi maha) me te pupuhitahi (e kīia nei ko te autofire), he maha ngā pupuhi i te wā e pupuri ana i te keu. Kāore i rite ki te nuinga o ngā whawhai pū pere, ko te pupuhitahi, ngā pupuhi rānei e puta ana i te taumata DEX o te kaiwhakaeke, kāore i te timatanga o te rauna whawhai i mua i ngā taumata DEX.

Ki tētahi whāinga kotahi, ko te pupuhi e whakanui ana i te tupono o te angitu mā te +20% me te pupuhitahi mā te +40%. Me kī e te kaiwhakaeke e hia ngā pupuhi e pupuhi ana (ko ngā pupuhi he toru ngā pupuhi, ko te pupuhitahi ka taea te whakaputa i te katoa o te mōkihi), me te whai i ngā matā i pau. Ka takahurihia te whakaeke i te tikanga tūturu. Mena he rahua te pupuhi, ka ngaro katoa ngā pupuhi i te pupuhi. Mena he angitu, ā, ka pā ki te whāinga, takahurihia he matā e hāngai ana i runga i te maha o ngā pupuhi i pupuhihia. Kia whiwhi i te tau rerekē, takahurihia he momo matā e hāngai ana, ā, wehea ki te rua (me whakamborotahi ki runga).

***Hei tauira,*** *mena e 8 ngā pupuhi i pupuhihia, takahurihia he D8; mena e 5 ngā pupuhi i pupuhihia, takahurihia he D10, ā, wehea ki te haurua, me whakamborotahi ki runga, me ētahi atu.*

Ka hiahiatia pea e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu kia puta ngā pupuhi katoa, ngā pupuhitahi rānei i roto i ngā tau me ngā ritenga matā, pēnei i te 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, me ētahi atu. Ko te otinga o tēnei takahuri matā ko te maha o ngā pupuhi i pā ki te whāinga. Ko te whakaeke tuatahi anake e taea ana te angitu motuhake, whakahirahira rānei—ko ngā toenga katoa he angitu tūturu.

Mō ngā whāinga maha, mena ka horahia ki te mara o te pupuhi, kāore e piki te whakatauranga pūkenga. Engari, ko ia whakaeke e whakamahi ana i te takahuri whakaeke kāore i whakarerekē mō ia whāinga. Ka taea e te kaiwhakaeke te tohatoha i te maha o ngā matā mō ia whāinga. I tēnei take, ka taea e ia whāinga te whiwhi i te angitu motuhake, whakahirahira rānei.

Mena he rerekē ngā whāinga ki tētahi mara pupuhi kuiti, pēnei i tētahi huarahi, i te rōpū tata rānei, ka piki te whakatauranga pūkenga o te kaiwhakaeke mā te +20% anō.

## Whakaeke Tuara me te Hoariri Awhina-kore

I waenganui o te whawhai ā-ringa, ka taea e tō pūāhua te whakaeke i te tuara kore-tiaki o tētahi whāinga, ko tērā whakaeke kotahi he *Māmā*. Mena ka angitu te whāinga i te takahuri Whakarongo, Rongo rānei *Uaua*, ka taea e rātou te hanga i tētahi takahuri Karo, parai rānei *Uaua*, engari mehemea he wātea anō mō te tiaki. Kāore he kino anō e tukuna e tēnei momo whakaeke.

## Whai (Chases)

Ka puta ngā whai ina hiahia ana tētahi taha ki te rere, ki te karo rānei i tētahi atu pūāhua, ahakoa ki te karo i te kino, ki te tuku rānei, ki te aukati rānei, ki te tīmata rānei i tētahi mahi. Nā te maha o ngā āhuatanga, ka mahia ngā whai i runga i te huarahi waitara, e tuku ana i te waitohu me te urupare o ngā kaitākaro me te kaiwhakahaere kēmu me te kore e hiahiatia he mahere, he pūnaha taipitopito rānei. Ahakoa i te rangi tuwhera me ngā waka rererangi, i ngā tiriti pukumahi o tētahi tāone nui, i roto rānei i ngā ana tawhito, kua pakaru i raro i te mata o te whenua, ka mahia ngā whai katoa me tēnei pūnaha.

I mua i te tīmatanga o te whai, me whakatau e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu ēnei āhuatanga e ono:

* **Ngā Tūnga Tīmata:** He painga nui rānei tētahi taha i te tīmatanga o te whai, ka tīmata rānei te whai i runga i te tūnga ōrite? Kei te tata rātou ki a rātou, kei tawhiti rānei?

* **Te Huarahi:** He pēhea te matatini, te mōrearea rānei o te huarahi, ā, e hia te kaha o ngā kaiwhai ki te whakarerekē i ngā tikanga? Ka huri rānei te taiao o te whai? He ētahi atu kaititiro kei te mōrearea, e whakaatu ana rānei i tō rātou ake mōrearea? He huinga o ngā taiao rerekē te huarahi? He mōrearea, he wero motuhake rānei te taiao?

* **Ngā Pūkenga e Hiahiatia ana:** He aha ngā pūkenga e taea te whakamahi i tēnei whai, pēnei i te Arai, te Rere, te Pairati rānei? He aha ngā mana? He aha ngā pūkenga e hiahiatia ana mena ka huri ngā tikanga o te whai? Mena ka he tō waka, e hiahia ana ētahi atu pūāhua ki te mahi kia rere tonu?

* **Whawhai:** Ka whakaekea rātou e ngā taha i te whai, ā, me ngā pū, ngā pūkenga rānei? I te nuinga o te wā, he pū pere ēnei, engari i te wā e taha ana, ka taea te whakamahi i ngā pū ā-ringa. He ētahi atu i te taiao e whakaatu ana i te riri?

* **Roanga:** He here rānei mō te roa o te whai, pēnei i te tawhiti tūturu i mua i te mutunga o te whai? He mahi rānei a te ngenge?

* **Otinga:** He aha te wāhi, te huringa o te āhuatanga, te tūnga rānei e mutu ai te whai? He mārama tēnei i te nuinga o te wā—engari me mārama i mua i te tīmatanga o te whai.

Ko te whai i te whai e whakamahi ana i te **ara awhe** (tirohia ki raro). Mena kei te takaro tahi, he pai te kape, te tā rānei i tēnei me te tuu ki runga i te tēpu, te tiri rānei i te atahanga mena kei te takaro ipurangi. Tautuhia ngā tūnga tīmata o ia kaiwhai i runga i te ara e tika ana ki a rātou anō, me te ‘roa’ e ōrite ana ki te kotahi MOV katoa i te tawhiti. Tohua ēnei tūnga ki ngā moni, ngā mea iti, ngā matā tāpiri, ētahi atu tohu rānei.

Ia rauna, me hanga e ngā kaiwhai katoa ngā takahuri e tika ana hei whakatau i te tawhiti e anga whakamua ana (ki te matau). Ko te angitu te tikanga kāore he anga whakamua e mahia ana, ko te angitu motuhake te tikanga ko te tangata i mua e nukuhia ana ki muri kotahi taahiraa, ko te angitu whakahirahira e nukuhia ana ki muri e rua taahiraa. Mena he rahua te takahuri, ko te pūāhua e takahuri ana e nuku ana ki muri kotahi mokowā, e rua rānei mena he hinga.

Ko te whāinga mō te rere i te whai, te tae rānei ki te wāhi, ki te ūnga rānei e hiahiatia ana i te tuatahi, ko te tae ki te tapawhā ‘Kāore i te Kitea’. Kia tae tētahi ki reira i te mutunga o te rauna, kua mutu te whai.

### Ngā Takahuri

Ko ngā takahuri e whakaaturia ana i te ripanga Whakarerekē Takahuri Pūkenga (ki tērā taha). I te wā e rahua ana te takahuri, takahurihia i runga i te ripanga Raruraru Whai (ki tērā taha).

Kāore tēnei e taea te mahi me ngā hōiho, me ngā waka e kumea ana e te hōiho. Ko te rahua e kore ai ia e nuku ki te huarahi e kore e hiahiatia, e haere tonu rānei i te huarahi kotahi.

* **Tukituki:** Ka tukituki te waka ki te whāinga, ka pā rānei. Ko te tukituki e tuku ana i te 1D6 kino mō ia taumata Tere ki te waka e tukituki ana ia, 1D3 mō ia taumata ki te waka e tukituki ana, ā, ko ngā hīkoi i roto i ia waka e tuku ana i te 1D3 kino mō ia taumata Tere. Ko te whakamarumaru whakahirahira (ngā nohoanga-rua, te pahuka pānga, me ētahi atu) ka taea te whakaiti i te kino i pāngia e ngā hīkoi mā te 3/4 (me whakamborotahi ki runga). Hanga he takahuri ātete SIZ vs SIZ mō ngā waka e rua, te whāinga rānei hei whakatau mena ka turakina tētahi i te rarangi, ki te taha rānei.

* **Rāmata (Ram):** Te tukituki i te waka ki tētahi arai i mua i a ia. Ko te whāinga e tuku ana i te 1D10 kino mō ia taumata Tere e haere ana te waka, 1D6 mō ia taumata ki te waka e tukituki ana, ā, 1D6 kino mō ia taumata Tere ki ngā hīkoi o ngā mea e rua (i roto rānei i te hanganga, mena i roto i te awhe o te pānga). Whakaritea tēnā ki te pūrua o te waka, te mea, te hanganga rānei, ā, toia te kino e toe ana mai i ōna tohu ora. Mena ka whakangarotia te whāinga, ka taea e te waka rāmata te haere tonu i roto i a ia. Hanga he takahuri ātete o SIZ vs SIZ mō te waka me tōna whāinga. Ko te toa te mea toa, ahakoa te haere tonu, te noho kore rānei, e tika ana.

* **Kino Waka:** Ka tukuna ngā tohu ora waka tauira. I te wā e tukituki ana te waka, e rāmatahia ana rānei, toia te uara pūrua o te waka, ā, toia te kino e toe ana mai i ōna tohu ora o nāianei. I te wā kua neke atu i te haurua o ōna tohu ora te waka, whakaititia tōna Tere mōrahi ki te haurua (me whakamborotahi ki runga). I te wā e 5 iti iho rānei ōna tohu ora, kāore te waka e mahi (ā, ka tukituki pea i tana whakamutunga). E whakaarohia ana i te whakangungu me te wheako o tētahi pūāhua kua whakamahia e rātou te pū hei parai i te wā o te hiahia, ā, kua ako mai i tērā. Ka taea tēnei te rerekē i te momo pū me te kāhua o te whakangungu.

***Hei tauira,*** *ko te whakangungu hōia tūturu i te whawhai ā-ringa he whakamahi i te pū hei karapu whakatairanga, tae atu ki te parai. Ko te hopu e kore e hiahiatia te parai me te pū.*

## Te Hinga (Knockback)

Ko te pā o tētahi pū ka taea te mahi nui atu i te kino ki tētahi whāinga. Ko te pā kaha e taea te tuku i tētahi whāinga ki te huri ki muri. E kīia ana he *hinga*. Mena ko tētahi whakaeke e tohua ana hei whakamātau hinga, ko te tapeke o te kino i mahia e whakaritea ana vs. te SIZ o te whāinga i roto i te takahuri ātete. Mena ka rahua te whāinga, ka hinga rātou ki muri mā te kotahi mita ia 5 ngā tohu o te kino e nui atu ana i tō rātou SIZ. Tāpiri atu ki ētahi atu kino i tangohia, me hanga e rātou he takahuri Kaha angitu kia tū tonu. Mena ko tētahi whakaeke e tutuki ana i te pānga motuhake hinga. Ka taea e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu te whakaheke i te tupono o te takahuri ātete kia whakaata i te kore-ōrite o te whenua, te heke, te taumata o mua o te kino kua pā kē, mena rānei he pūāhua kei te rererangi, pēnei i te rere.

Mena ko te pūāhua e whāia ana e eke ana, kei runga rānei i tētahi waka, me whakamātau e rātou he takahuri Arai, Pairati, Eke rānei, e tika ana. Mena ka angitu, ko te SIZ o te mea e ekehia ana, o te waka rānei ka tāpirihia ki ō rātou ake SIZ. Mena ka rahua, ko te hinga ka whakatauria anake ki te SIZ o te pūāhua, ā, ka taea te hinga i tō rātou kararehe, waka rānei, ahakoa ka taea e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu te whakarerekē tēnei i runga i ngā huānga pēnei i te here, ngā whitiki noho, te whirihoranga o te nohoanga, me ētahi atu.

Ka taea e tētahi kaiwhakaeke te kī ko tōna whakaeke i ahu mai i runga tonu i te whāinga, e pana ana i te whāinga ki te whenua. I tēnei take, kāore te whāinga i te hinga, engari me hanga he takahuri Kaha kia tū tonu. Mena ka rahua tēnei takahuri, ka hinga rātou **Takoto**. I te nuinga o te wā ko te hinga mai i te tūnga tū kāore e puta he kino, engari mena he roa ake te tawhiti, whakamahia ngā ture mō te **Hinga**.

I tētahi wāhi e mahia ana ngā hinga i ngā wā katoa, pēnei me ngā toa nui, ngā toa toi hoia kaha rānei, ka panui pea te kaiwhakahaere kēmu ko tētahi pā angitu he tupono mō te hinga, ahakoa i tuku kino rānei ki te whāinga, kāore rānei. I tēnei take, ko te paepae hinga ka iti ake pea, pēnei i te 1/2 o te SIZ o te whāinga, kia piki ai tōna auautanga.

## Whakaeke Whakamomori (Knockout Attacks)

Ko ngā whakaeke whakamomori e mahi ana anake ki ngā Tangata me ngā Humanoids, ētahi atu mea ora rānei me ngā upoko kua mārama te tautuhi. Kia noho kore-mōhio te whāinga, whakapuakihia tō pūāhua hiahia i te timatanga o te rauna. Ko te takahuri whakaeke *Uaua* ka mahia pēnei i te whāinga i tētahi wāhanga tinana motuhake (pēnei i te upoko). Ko te whakaeke he kore-mate, ā, kāore i te whakaaro ki te tuku kino, ahakoa e takahurihia ana te kino hei whakatau i te tupono mō te whakamomori. Ko te pūrua e tiaki ana i te tikanga tūturu i ngā wā katoa. Mena ko te kino e ōrite ana ki te whara iti, ko te kino tūturu i takahurihia e warewarehia, ā, ko te whāinga e tukuna te kino mōrahi mō te pū (whai muri i te pūrua) engari kāore i te whakamomori. Mena ko te whakaeke e angitu ana, ā, ko te kino i takahurihia e ōrite ana ki te whara nui (whai muri i te pūrua), ko te whāinga e tango i te 1 kino, ā, ka whakamomoria mō ngā rauna 1D10+10 (ka takahuri ngaro te kaiwhakahaere kēmu mō tēnei).

Ko ngā pānga o ngā angitu motuhake, whakahirahira rānei (pēnei i te kino tāpiri, te karo i te pūrua) e pā ana i ngā wā katoa, i te wā ko ētahi atu pānga motuhake (kino tapahi, hinga, me ētahi atu) kāore e pā ki ngā whakamātau whakamomori.

## Te Puna Mārama

Ko ngā rama, ngā rama-a-ringa, ngā rama-rere, ngā rama-kōrere, me ētahi atu taputapu e tuku ana i ngā puna mārama, ā, e tuku ana i te mārama nui kia pānui ai, kia whakamahi ai i ngā pūkenga Rapu, Titiro rānei. Ko ngā rama me ngā rama-a-ringa e tuku ana i te kitenga (ā, e kitea ana) mai i te 100 mita te tawhiti i te pōuri. I te 100 mita, me angitu tō pūāhua i te takahuri Titiro kia kite i te rama, i tētahi puna mārama e ōrite ana rānei. Ko ētahi atu puna mārama ka taea te whānui mai i te nui atu i ēnei, ka taka rānei i waenganui (tirohia te **Pōuri**).

Ko te rama e kōrapa ana, he ngāwari ki te maka atu, he ngāwari ki te pupuhi atu, he iti ake mena kei roto i te rama-a-ringa. Ko te rama ware-roa e kā ana mō te ono hāora pea; ko te rama-pōti-poto e kā ana mō te rua hāora. Ko te rama-a-ringa e tuku ana i te mura nui, mārama, ā, he uaua ki te pupuhi atu. Ko te rama-a-ringa e kā ana mō te kotahi hāora pea. Ko ngā rama hinu, rama-a-ringa rānei e mau ana i te mārama mō te maha o ngā hāora. Mena ka maka atu, ko tētahi puna mārama e kā ana e haere tonu ana me te takahuri Waimarie angitu, ā, ka puta pea he mōrearea i te whawhai (tirohia te *Mātao me te Wera*). Mena me whakamahi te puna mārama me te tupato (te rama hinu, me ētahi atu), ko ngā pūkenga e hāngai ana he *Uaua*.

He maha ngā mana ka taea te tuku mārama (tirohia ō rātou whakaurunga i te *Upoko Whā: Ngā Mana*).

## Whawhai Taua

I ētahi wā ka uru tō pūāhua ki tētahi **whawhai taua**, he mahi taktika e whai wāhi ana te maha atu o ngā kaiwhai i te maha o ngā pūāhua kaitākaro. Mena e hiahia ana tō kaiwhakahaere kēmu ki te whakaatu i tētahi whawhai taua e kore e mōhiotia te otinga, ka taea tēnei te mahi mā te raupapa o ngā takahuri Rautaki i te taha o ngā taha e whakahē ana; he raupapa o ngā takahuri ātete me ia taha e tohu ana me tētahi uara POT i whakaritea e te GM; tētahi whawhai waitara rānei i waenganui i ngā kaiwhawhai tokorua e whakaatu ana i ngā tohu ora o ngā hōia.

I te nuinga o te wā, kua whakatau kē te kaiwhakahaere kēmu i te otinga o te whawhai taua, ā, e whakauru ana i te whawhai hei huānga whakaari, e tuku ana i tō pūāhua he tupono ki te whai wāhi ki te riri i te taumata e tika ana, mai i te papa whawhai ake, te rūma whawhai i te tāone nui, i runga rānei i te piriti o tētahi waka nui.

I ēnei wā, me whakaatu te kaiwhakahaere kēmu i te whawhai mā te kōrero, e whakarāpopoto ana i ngā wāhi nui e tika ana, ā, tuku i ngā wāhanga e taea ai e ngā pūāhua kaitākaro te whai wāhi ki tētahi, neke atu rānei ngā whawhai iti. I ngā wā e rumakina ana ō pūāhua ki te whawhai, me hanga e ia pūāhua e whai wāhi ana he takahuri Waimarie ia rauna (e rima meneti). Ko te angitu te tikanga kāore he kino i tangohia. Ko te rahua te tikanga e 1D6 ngā tohu kino i tangohia. Ko te hinga te tikanga e 2D6 ngā tohu kino i tangohia. Ko te pūrua e whakaiti ana i tēnei kino.

## Pupuhia i te wā e Neke ana

Ka taea e tō pūāhua te whakamātau i te pupuhi pū pere i te wā e eke ana, e arai ana, e pairati ana i te kararehe eke, waka rānei, engari ko ngā pūkenga whawhai e hāngai ana he here ki te whakatauranga pūkenga mō te kararehe eke, waka rānei e whakahaere ana rātou. Ko te whakaeke i te wā e rere ana he here ki te takahuri Kaha o tō pūāhua, ko ngā whakaeke rānei i te wā e peke ana i te rangi ka taea te whakamahi i te pūkenga Peke hei pōtae. Me whakatau e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu mena he tupono te whakaeke, he waho rānei i te awhe o te tupono. Ko ētahi tautuhinga e hē ana i te taha o te mahi nui (tirohia ngā **Tikanga Whawhai Rerekē**).

Ko te neke me te maka i tētahi pū pere a-ringa (tao, toki, maripi, kōhatu, me ētahi atu) i tētahi pū rānei e anga ana ki mua ka taea te whakamātau i te whakatauranga pūkenga tūturu. Ko te neke me te maka, te pupuhi rānei ki te taha e hanga ana i te whakaeke *Uaua* nā te whakararuraru o te neke ki tētahi huarahi i tua atu i te mea e whāia ana. Ko ngā kopere he māmā ki te pupuhi ki te taha, ā, kāore e whiu. Ko ngā whakaeke i muri tonu i tētahi pūāhua pūkoro he 1/5 o te whakatauranga pūkenga tūturu (e ōrite ana ki te angitu motuhake), ā, me hiahia he Kaha, he Eke, he Arai rānei, e tika ana, kia whakamātau. I te mea i tuhia i roto i te *Upoko Ono: Whawhai*, ka taea e tō pūāhua te neke mō te haurua o te rauna whawhai, kātahi ka maka, ka pupuhi, ka kopere i te kopere i te haurua, i te taumata DEX e tika ana.

## Whawhai i runga Hōiho

Ko te whawhai i te wā e eke ana he rite ki te whawhai i runga i te wae, ahakoa he maha ngā tikanga e whakarerekē ana i te wheako mō ngā taha katoa e whai wāhi ana. Mena ko te kararehe eke he motopaika, he waka e ōrite ana rānei, me whakakapi te pūkenga Arai ki te pūkenga Eke. I raro nei ko ngā whakarerekē ki te whawhai e whai wāhi ana ngā kararehe eke:

* I te wā e eke ana, ko ngā whakatauranga pūkenga whawhai o te kaieke he here ki te whakatauranga pūkenga o tō rātou pūkenga Eke.

* Ko ētahi momo pū kāore e taea te whakamahi i te wā e eke ana, pēnei i ngā pū-pou ringa-rua me/rānei ngā pākete nui.

* I te wā e eke ana, ko ngā Karo katoa e whakamātau ana te kaieke he *Uaua*.
* Ko tētahi pūāhua e eke ana e whakamahi ana i te MOV o te kararehe eke mō tētahi nekehanga i te rauna, e whakaaro ana e hanga ana rātou i te takahuri Eke angitu.

* Ko tētahi pūāhua e eke ana kei roto i tētahi **Tūnga Kaha** ki ngā whāinga i te whenua.

* Ko tētahi whakaeke hinga angitu e tango ana i tētahi whāinga e eke ana, pēnei i te whara nui. Ka taea e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu te whakatau ko ētahi atu angitu motuhake (pēnei i te whakararu) ka taea hoki te tango kore-hiahia.

* I te wā e hinga ana mai i te kararehe eke, me hanga e te kaieke he takahuri Waimarie angitu, ka tango rānei i te 1D6 kino (ki tētahi wāhi pā matapōkere, mena ka whakamahia). Ko te pūrua e tiaki ana i te tikanga tūturu, engari mena ka hinga te takahuri Waimarie, ka hopukia rātou i tō rātou nohoanga, ka toia mō te 1D3 ia rauna whawhai, ka tango kino ia rauna, tae noa ki te tuku i a rātou anō me te takahuri Kaha *Uaua*. I ngā āhuatanga katoa, me hanga e te pūāhua he takahuri Manawanui angitu, ka noho kore-mōhio rānei mō te rauna whawhai e whai ake nei (tirohia te **Te Whakatenetene, te Whakamaura**).

* Me heke te kaieke ki te tiki i tētahi pū i hinga, i maka rānei. Ko te heke he mahi koreutu, ahakoa ko te eke anō he mahi nekehanga haurua.

* Ko ngā whakaeke utu e puta ana i te mutunga o te nekehanga katoa. I te wā e utu ana, e mau ana i te pū, me whakamahi tō pūāhua i te whakarerekē kino mō te kararehe eke hei utu mō tō rātou ake. I te nuinga o te wā, ko te whakaeke utu e hiahia ana he takahuri pūkenga Eke (Arai rānei) angitu hei whakahaere i te kararehe eke ki te huarahi e tika ana.

Ko tētahi huarahi tiaki ki te utu eke he tao, he tao, he pū-pou, he pū e ōrite ana rānei, e mau ana me te kōtara i te whenua, e kore e taea te neke ki tētahi utu. Ko te whakaeke e takahurihia ana i te tikanga tūturu, mena ka angitu, ko te whakarerekē kino o te kararehe eke e utu ana ka tāpirihia ki te kino o te pū kua whakatōia. Kia karo i tētahi pū kua whakatōia, me whakakore te kaieke i tētahi mahi whawhai, me hanga he takahuri Eke (Arai rānei) kia karo i te pā. Mena ko te kararehe eke he mohio, kāore e hiahia ki te oma ki runga i tētahi pū e nohoia ana, ka taea e ia te whakamahi i tōna ake pūkenga Karo i te whakahē o te hiahia o te pūāhua e eke ana. Me hiahia pea he kaieke he takahuri Eke angitu me te takahuri ātete POW vs. POW angitu kia akiaki i tētahi kararehe eke ki te whara i a ia anō. Ko te rahua o tētahi o ēnei te tikanga ka Karo te kararehe eke, ka hoki rānei i ētahi atu mahi mō te toenga o te rauna.

Mena ka whakamahia ngā wāhi pā, ka taea e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu te kōwhiri kia kore e taea ētahi wāhi pā i waenganui i tētahi e eke ana me te mea e eke ana, i runga i ngā pū e whakamahia ana. Mō tētahi pūāhua kāore i te eke, ko ngā pū a-ringa iti kāore pea e taea te tae ki te upoko o te whāinga e eke ana, i te wā ko ngā pū roa kāore he here pēnei. Mena e tika ana, takahurihia ētahi otinga o te 'upoko' i te takahuri wāhi pā. Ko tētahi pūāhua e eke ana e mau ana i tētahi pū a-ringa o te rahi waenga kāore pea e taea te patu i te kopu o raro, i ngā waewae rānei o tētahi pūāhua kāore i te eke. Mena e tika ana, ko tētahi pūāhua e eke ana e whakaeke ana i tētahi pūāhua kāore i te eke ka takahuri 1D10+10 mō ngā wāhi pā.

Tāpiri atu, i runga i te tūnga o te pūāhua e eke ana me te pūāhua kāore i te eke, ko ētahi wāhi pā (matau/maui) kāore pea e taea. Me whakaaro te kaiwhakahaere kēmu ko ēnei whakaeke i pā ki te kararehe eke anake. E rua ngā pūāhua e eke ana e whawhai ana he here matau/maui anake ō rātou, ā, he tino taea te whakaeke i a rātou anō.

## Ngā Pū Taiao

Ko ngā pū taiao he wāhanga o te tinana o te kaiwhakaeke.

***Hei tauira,*** *ko te ringa me te waewae o te Tangata he pū taiao mō rātou, ā, ka taea e tō pūāhua te whakamahi i ngā ringa, tae noa ki ngā waewae ki te Hopu i tētahi hoariri.*

Ko te nuinga o ētahi atu mea ora he pū taiao pēnei i ngā karawhiu, ngā niho, ngā hiku hopu, ngā wero, me ngā kōtara. He pū pere taiao ētahi. I te mea i tuhia i roto i te *Upoko Tekau Mā Tahi: Ngā Mea Ora*, ko te nuinga o ngā mea ora e mau ana he 1D6 kino ki ngā karawhiu me te 1D3 kino ki te ngau, ki te kōtara rānei, tāpiri atu ki te whakarerekē kino.

Ka taea e tō pūāhua te whakamahi i ō rātou pū taiao i te huarahi ka taea e rātou te whakamahi i ngā pū tūturu, te whakaeke me te parai i tētahi whāinga. Mena kei te whakamahia ngā wāhi pā, ko ngā tohu ora mō te parai ringa he tohu ora o tō pūāhua i te wāhi pā o te ringa me ngā mea katoa e nui atu ana i ngā tohu pūrua o te ringa ka haere tūturu ki ngā tohu ora o te wāhi o te ringa o tō pūāhua me ō rātou tohu ora tapeke.

Ko te nuinga o ngā mea ora me ngā pū taiao pēnei i ngā karawhiu, ngā wero, me ētahi atu e whakamahi ana i a rātou pēnei i te Tangata e whakamahi ana i te pū, me ngā taumata DEX ōrite me ngā whiringa ōrite pēnei i te hopu me te Karo. He iti noa ngā kararehe mohoao e mōhio ana ki te parai i ngā whakaeke, i te mea kāore i te tikanga tūturu. Me whakatau e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu mena ka whakamātau te mea ora ki te parai, ki te Karo rānei, i runga i te INT, DEX, me tōna tikanga whawhai o te kararehe.

## Awhe-kore (Point-blank)

Ko te tupono o tō pūāhua ki te pā ki te pū pere he *Māmā* i te wā ko te awhe ki te whāinga he iti iho, he ōrite rānei ki tō rātou DEX/3 i roto i ngā mita. Kia tae te awhe i waenganui i te kaiwhakaeke me te whāinga ki te awhe pā (kotahi mita mō te nuinga o ngā pū, e rua mita mō ngā pū-pou, tao, me ētahi atu), ka huri te whawhai ki te whawhai ā-ringa. Ko ētahi pū (kopere, pū maka, me ētahi atu) kāore he hua i te awhe whawhai ā-ringa, ka noho *Uaua*, i te wā ko ētahi pū (pū, pū pūngao, pū-pou, me ētahi atu) he *Māmā* i te awhe-kore.

I te awhe-kore, ka whakaaetia ngā parai ki ngā pū pere, i te mea ka taea e te kaiwawao te whakamātau ki te aukati i tētahi ringa e tohu ana ki a rātou, ki te whakahaere rānei i tētahi pū pere.

## Wero (Poison)

Ko ngā paitini katoa he uara kaha (POT) e whakaritea ana ki te CON o te pūāhua i paitinihia. Mena ka hinga te paitini i te CON o tō pūāhua, ko tōna POT katoa e tukuna hei kino ki ngā tohu ora. Mena kāore te paitini i te hinga i te CON o te pūāhua, he iti ake tōna pānga—i te nuinga o te wā ko te haurua anake o te POT o te paitini i te kino (me whakamborotahi ki runga). Tirohia ngā Paitini Tauira.

Ko te kino paitini he kino tūturu ki ngā tohu ora, ki tētahi āhuatanga rānei. Ko te kino paitini kāore i te puta i te rauna whawhai kotahi i paitinihia ai tō pūāhua. Ko te whakaroa i mua i te pānga o te kino paitini e whakawhirinaki ana ki te paitini. Hāunga mena i tohua e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu, ko te whakaroa he toru ngā rauna whawhai mō ngā paitini kaha, e toru rānei ngā rauna katoa mō ngā paitini pōturi. Ka hiahia pea tō kaiwhakahaere kēmu ki te whakarite i ngā ture matatini e whai wāhi ana ki te paitini i tukuna ki ngā wāhi pā, ngā wā tīmata rerekē, he huarahi matatini rānei.

Mena ka tango tō pūāhua i ngā rongoā e rua o tētahi paitini, me hanga e rātou he takahuri ātete motuhake vs. ia mea: ko ngā rongoā e rua o tētahi paitini POT 10 kāore i te ōrite ki tētahi rongoā o tētahi paitini POT 20. Ko ētahi mana, taputapu rānei ka taea te tiaki i te paitini: tirohia te Upoko Whā: Ngā Mana me te Upoko Waru: Ngā Taputapu mō ngā pārongo.

## Rongoā Paitini

Ko te nuinga o ngā paitini katoa he rongoā. Ko ngā rongoā katoa he whakatauranga POT, pēnei ki ngā paitini. ## Takoto (Prone)

Kei te takoto tō pūāhua i runga i te whenua, ahakoa i runga i te hiahia, i te kore hiahia rānei. Ko ngā āhuatanga e whai ake nei e pā ana ki te pūāhua takoto (tirohia hoki te pānga motuhake hinga,):

* Ko ngā whakaeke pū pere katoa ā-ringa, waenga-awhe rānei ki tētahi pūāhua takoto he Māmā

* Ko ngā whakaeke pū pere awhe-roa katoa ki tētahi pūāhua takoto he *Uaua*.

* Ko ngā whakaeke, ngā parai, me ngā Karo a te pūāhua takoto he *Uaua*.

* Ko ngā whakaeke a tētahi pūāhua takoto e mau ana i te pū ringa-rua he *Uaua*.

* Ko tētahi pūāhua takoto kāore e taea te tāpiri i tētahi whakarerekē kino pai ki ngā whakaeke, ahakoa ko ngā whakarerekē kino kino e pā tonu ana.

* Ka taea e tētahi pūāhua takoto te whakamahi i ngā pū pere pēnei i ngā pū, ngā pū-pou, ngā pū pūngao rānei i tō rātou whakatauranga pūkenga katoa. Ko ētahi kāhua kopere e tuku ana i te whakamahi takoto, ahakoa i te wā e takoto ana te kaiwhaka kopere i tōna tuara. Heoi, ko ētahi pū kāore e taea te pupuhi tūturu i te wā e takoto ana, i te whakaaro o te kaiwhakahaere kēmu. Mena kei te ruarua, ko ngā whakaeke me noho *Uaua*.

Ka taea e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu te tuku i te whakarerekē +10% i te wā e whakamahi ana i ngā pū, ētahi atu pū pere a-whaiaro rānei i te wā e takoto ana i runga i te hiahia, hei whakaatu i te pūmautanga tāpiri. Ka taea tēnei whakarerekē te hoatu tāpiri ki ētahi whakarerekē mō ngā awhe, me ētahi atu.

Kāore he takahuri hei whakatika anō i muri i te takoto, engari ko tēnei mahi he wāhi o te whakaeke, e hanga ana i ngā parai me ngā Karo katoa he *Uaua*, ā, ko ngā whakaeke katoa ki te pūāhua i te wā e whakatika ana he *Māmā*. Ko te takahuri Karo *Uaua* angitu e whakahoki ana i tētahi pūāhua takoto ki ōna waewae i tōna taumata DEX ā, ka tuku tētahi mahi whakaeke i te rauna whawhai.

I te wā kē, ko te huinga o ngā takahuri Karo me Kaha angitu e tutuki ana i te mahi ōrite, e karo ana i te whakarerekē *Māmā* ki te whakaekea, engari kāore e tuku tētahi mahi whakaeke (ko te takahuri Kaha e tango ana i te wāhi o te mahi whakaeke).

## Te Whakaheke i te Mana o te Whiu (Pulling Blows)

E whakaarohia ana ko tētahi whakaeke e mahia ana me te hiahia ki te tuku kino i te mea e taea ana, engari mena e hiahiatia ana, ka taea e tō pūāhua te ‘whakaheke’ i tētahi whakaeke, e kōwhiri ana ki te tuku kino iti mā te patu māmā, mā te kore rānei e patu i ngā wāhanga tinana hira. ***Hei tauira,*** *ko te pū-a-ringa e tuku ana i te 4D6 i te kino ka taea te whakaiti i te kino ki te 4D4, 4D3, 4D2 rānei, ka taea rānei te whakaiti ki te 3D6, 2D6, 1D6 rānei.*

Mena he pātai mō te whakaiti i te uara o ngā matā, ko tō rātou raupapa, mai i te mea iti ki te mea nui: D2, D3, D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, ki te D20.

## Irirangi (Radiation)

Ko te whakapā ki te irirangi, ki ngā rawa irirangi rānei he tikanga kino ki ngā mea ora. Ko te pānga o te paitini irirangi (acute radiation syndrome) he ōrite ki te paitini me ngā wera tūturu. Ahakoa ko te kaha o te irirangi e ine ana i ngā **rads**, i konei e kāwai ana i te kaha-iti ki te kaha-mate (tirohia te ripanga ki raro) me ngā POT hono. Me whakarite e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu tētahi kaha (POT) ki ia whakapā mō te whakamahi i te takahuri ātete. Mena ka pā tō pūāhua ki te irirangi, me hanga e rātou he takahuri ātete mā te whakamahi CON vs. te POT o te irirangi. Ko te angitu te tikanga ka karo tō pūāhua i ngā pānga mō te wā i rārangitia. Ko te **Ripanga Pānga Irirangi** (ki raro) e tuku ana i ngā aratohu mō ngā taumata POT rerekē o te irirangi.

Ko te mate irirangi acute he tino mate. He iti noa ngā huarahi hei whakaiti i te whakapā ki te irirangi i te ao hou. Ko te Āwhina Tuatahi he kore hua ki te irirangi, ā, ko te pūkenga Rongoā kāore e taea te mahi nui ki te pupuri i ōna pānga. Ko ngā mana me ngā taputapu rerekē ka taea te tiaki i ngā pānga o te irirangi: tirohia te *Upoko Whā: Ngā Mana* me te *Upoko Waru: Ngā Taputapu* mō ngā pārongo, ā, ko ngā tautuhinga o muri mai ka whai huarahi pea ki te mahi i a rātou mā ngā huarahi matū, hangarau rānei.

Ko te maimoatanga ki runga o te irirangi e hāngai ana ki tētahi tauira taumata mana tūturu o ōna pānga whakamataku. Ko ngā momo toa nui me te ao-i-muri-i-te-aitua he irirangi hei puna o te whanaketanga painga; hei utu mō ngā mate pukupuku mate me te kiko irirangi, ko te whakapā ki te irirangi e puta ana ngā whanaketanga mana-nui, e whakatere ana rānei i te whanaketanga o ētahi momo. Ka taea e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu te tuku i tētahi pūāhua i tētahi tupono POW×1 (nui atu rānei) o tētahi o ēnei whanaketanga painga mai i te whakapā ki te irirangi, ka whai wāhi rānei te whakapā ki te irirangi ki te takenga mai o tō pūāhua, e tuku ana i a rātou he mana-nui.

## Te Pupuhi ki Runga, ki Raro rānei

Mena kei te pupuhi i tētahi pū pere ki tētahi whāinga, i tētahi koki neke atu i te 45°, ko te whakaeke he *Uaua*. ## Te Whakatenetene, te Whakamaura

Ka hiahia pea tō pūāhua ki te whakamaura i tētahi hoariri hei utu mō te patu i a rātou. Ko te noho whakamaura he wheako kōhukihuki, raruraru, i te wā e kanikani ana ngā whetū i ngā kanohi o te whāinga, ā, e kaukau ana ō rātou upoko i te mamae o te pā ohorere. Ko tētahi whāinga whakamaura kāore e taea te whakaeke i te wā e whakamaura ana, ā, ka taea anake te whakamātau ki te Karo, te parai rānei i tētahi whakaeke mena ka hanga e rātou he takahuri Whakaaro angitu mō ia whakamātau. Tāpiri atu, ko ngā whakaeke katoa ki te whāinga he *Māmā*. Ko te whāinga whakamaura ka taea te whakamātau ki te rere, engari kia mahi tēnei me hiahia he takahuri Whakaaro angitu kia kite i tētahi huarahi rere me te takahuri Kaha angitu kia wiri ki waho i te riri.

Whakamaura, whakamaura rānei i tētahi pūāhua mā tētahi o ēnei huarahi:

* Whakamahia te pānga motuhake kuru e whakaaturia ana i te *Upoko Ono: Whawhai*.
* Whakamahia tētahi pū, tētahi mea kōhatu rānei hei whakamaura i tētahi whāinga (tirohia te **Whakaeke Whakamomori**).

* Whakamahia tētahi pū i whakaritea hei whakamaura (whakamaura rānei) i tētahi hoariri hei utu mō te whara i a rātou. Hanga tuatahi he whakaeke whāinga tūturu. I te whakaeke angitu, takahurihia te kino tūturu o te pū, toia te pūrua, me te hanga i tētahi takahuri ātete vs. ngā tohu ora o nāianei o te whāinga. Mena ka angitu, ko te whāinga e whakamaura ana mō ngā rauna e ōrite ana ki ngā tohu o te kino i takahurihia. Ko tētahi otinga whakahirahira e tuku ana i te kino mōrahi i takahurihia tāpiri atu ki te kino tūturu i takahurihia. Mena ko tētahi pū whakamaura e tutuki ana i te otinga motuhake, tatau te kino mōrahi o te pū hei utu mō te takahuri. Mena ka rahua te whakaeke, ko te whāinga kāore e tango i tētahi kino, ā, kāore i te whakamaura. Ko te whāinga whakamaura e tango anake i te kino iti rawa i takahurihia mō te pū. Mena ko te kino i takahurihia i mua e whakaiti ana i te whāinga ki te 1, 2 rānei ngā tohu ora, ko te whāinga e whakamomoria ana hei utu mō te whakamaura. Mena ka whakaiti i a rātou ki te 0 tohu ora, ka mate pea rātou i te kōhukihuki hāunga mena ka whiwhi aro hauora.

* Ko tētahi whāinga ka taea te whakakore me te pūkenga Hopu, te whakamahi rānei i tētahi pū me te pānga motuhake whakararu.

Ko te pūrua e tiaki ana i te kino whakamaura hāunga mena ko te whakaeke e whāia ana i runga i te upoko kore-pūrua. Ko ngā pū pūngao, ētahi atu pū rānei e tuku ana i te kino whakamaura e warewarehia ana ngā pūrua tūturu, onamata, waenga rānei i ngā wā katoa, ahakoa ka taea e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu te kī ko ngā pū whakamaura i runga i te hiko e mahi ana i te kaha katoa vs. ngā whāinga i te pūrua konganuku. Ko te pūrua matatini e tiakina ana i te nuinga o te wā i ngā pū whakamaura i runga i te pūngao me te mahi i te kaha katoa ki a rātou.

## Ngā Tūnga Nui/Iti

Mena kei te tū tō pūāhua i te iti rawa te haurua-nui ake (nui atu rānei) i tōna whāinga, he tūnga kaha tō rātou, he painga i runga i tō rātou hoariri.

Me aromātai te kaiwhakahaere kēmu i te maha o ngā kaiwhakaeke me ngā kaiwawao, te tirotiro i te whenua, i te taiao rānei, me te whakaaro i ngā pū e whakamahia ana i te wā e whakatau ana e hia ngā pūāhua e taea te whakaeke i tētahi whāinga kotahi i te rauna. Ko ngā pūāhua e toe ana e pana ana, ā, kāore e taea te whakaeke engari ka taea te mahi i ētahi atu mahi, mena e hiahiatia ana. E whakaaro ana tēnei i ngā pūāhua rahi-Tangata: ka taea e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu te whakarerekē i ēnei tau mō ngā pūāhua nui, iti rānei.

## Whakaeke Horo (Sweep)

Ko tētahi hoariri nui, tētahi pūāhua rānei e whakaeke ana me tētahi pū nui, roa rānei ka taea te whakamātau i tētahi **whakaeke horo**, tētahi piu whakapae puta noa i tētahi rohe nui kia patu pea i ngā hoariri maha. Ko tēnei whakaeke he tupono nui ki te angitu engari he iti ake te kino i tētahi whakaeke tūturu. Ko te whakaeke horo i mahia me tētahi pū e tika ana ki tētahi pūāhua e tū ana i te rohe whāinga he Māmā, engari ko te kino he haurua (me whakamborotahi ki runga). Ko tētahi mea ora me tētahi whakaeke horo taiao me whakamahi i te whakatauranga pūkenga me te kino i rārangitia i roto i tōna whakamāramatanga, kāore i te whakarerekē.

Kia karo i tētahi whakaeke horo, kāore e taea e tō pūāhua te Karo, engari me whakamahi i te pūkenga Peke kia haere i te huarahi. Ko te parai i tētahi whakaeke horo he mea taea, ahakoa me hanga e te pūāhua parai he takahuri ātete o tō rātou STR vs. te kino o te whakaeke (i mua i te haurua). Ko te rahua te tikanga e pā ana rātou, i te wā ko te parai angitu e aukati ana i te horo, e whakaora ana i ētahi atu whāinga pea i te huarahi o te horo.

Ko tētahi whakaeke angitu me te whakaeke horo mā te whakamahi i tētahi pū kore-mata e tuku pea i te pānga hinga mō te tangata i pā ki te rohe o te pānga o te whakaeke horo (tirohia te **Te Hinga**). Ko ngā hinga e takahurihia ana mō ia pūāhua i roto i te awhe o te pānga o te whakaeke horo, mai i te mea tuatahi i pā ki te mea whakamutunga. Mena ko tētahi pūāhua e angitu ana ki te ātete i te hinga, kāore tētahi o ngā pūāhua e toe ana i te pāngia.

Ko ngā whakaeke horo e taea anake te tuku i ngā pānga motuhake kuru, hinga, tapahi rānei, e tika ana. Ko ngā werohanga me ngā whakararu kāore i te otinga o te horo. Mena ko tētahi pānga motuhake werohanga, whakararu rānei e takahurihia ana, ka taea e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu te whakakapi i te otinga ki tētahi pānga motuhake kuru, hinga, tapahi rānei e tika ana, te whakaaro rānei he angitu tūturu.

## Ngā Pū Maka (Thrown Weapons)

I te nuinga o te wā, ko ngā pū he mea hanga mō te whawhai ā-ringa, mō te maka rānei, he uaua kia rua. Heoi, i te kōhukihuki o te whawhai, i ētahi wā ka hiahiatia te maka i tētahi pū kāore i te whakaaro kia maka, te whakamahi rānei i tētahi pū maka i te whawhai ā-ringa. He iti noa ngā pū, pēnei i te hatchet me te tao, he rite tonu te hua i ngā āhuatanga e rua. I te wā e whakamahi ana i ngā pū maka, ko te whakarerekē kino o tō pūāhua he haurua (me whakamborotahi ki runga). Mena he maha ngā matā o te whakarerekē kino o tō pūāhua, ka haurua ia mea, hei utu mō te haurua o te maha o ngā matā.

Ko ngā pū i whakaarohia mō te maka he ngāwari ake i ngā pū whawhai ā-ringa, ā, kāore i te taurite tūturu mō te whawhai ā-ringa. Mena ka akiakina tō pūāhua ki te whawhai ā-ringa e mau ana i tētahi pū maka kāore ō rātou pūkenga pū whawhai ā-ringa mō tērā, ko ngā whakaeke me tērā pū he *Uaua*.

## E Rua ngā Pū

Ka taea e tō pūāhua te whakamahi i ngā pū whawhai ā-ringa e rua (pū pere iti rānei) i te wā kotahi, hei utu mō te pū kotahi, tētahi pū me tētahi pākete rānei. Kāore ngā pū e hiahia ana kia rite te momo, te kāwai rānei. Hei tauira, ka taea e tō pūāhua te whawhai me tētahi kōtara i ia ringa, i tētahi hoari rānei i tētahi ringa me tētahi pū i tētahi atu. Kāore he painga ki te ringa-kaha. Ko te whawhai me ngā pū e rua i te wā kotahi he tikanga e whai ake nei:

* Ko te whakamahi i ngā pū e rua i te whawhai kāore e tuku aunoa i tētahi whakaeke tuarua, he maha atu rānei ngā parai i ērā e whakaaetia ana i te tikanga tūturu.

* Mena he pūkenga 100% neke atu rānei o tō pūāhua i ia pū e whakamahia ana, ka taea e rātou te wehe i te whakaeke pēnei i ngā ture kōwhiri mō ngā pūkenga neke atu i te 100%. I tēnei take, ko ia whakaeke he *Uaua*, me te whakaeke anō mō ia pū e warewarehia ana.
  ***Hei tauira,*** *ko tō pūāhua he rapier (me tētahi 102% pūkenga) i tōna ringa matau me tētahi kōtara (110% i tērā atu) i tōna ringa mauī. Mena ka wehea e ia ōna whakatauranga pūkenga i tēnei rauna me te whakaeke i ngā wā maha, ko tōna whakaeke rapier he 51% me tōna whakaeke kōtara he 55%. Ko te whakaeke rapier tuarua me te whakaeke kōtara tuarua e warewarehia ana, i te mea ko te whakaeke kōtara e tango ana i te wāhi o te whakaeke rapier tuarua.*

* Mena ka whakaaetia tētahi whakaeke tuarua, ka puta i te 5 ngā taumata DEX i muri i te whakaeke tuatahi.

* Ko ngā whakaeke me te ringa tuarua he *Uaua* mena kāore tō pūāhua i whakangungua i te whakamahinga o te whawhai mā te whakamahi i taua ringa (tirohia ki raro).
* Mena ko te pū e whakamahia ana i te tikanga tūturu i te ringa tuarua (pēnei i te kōtara parai, i te pākete rānei), i tō pūāhua rānei he pūkenga 100% neke atu rānei me te pū, kāore rātou e tango i tēnei whiu.

* Ko tētahi painga tuatahi ki te whawhai me ngā pū e rua ko te kore e kore he tiaki o tō pūāhua mena ka ngaro tētahi pū i a rātou nā te pakaru, i te hinga rānei.

* Ko te whawhai me tētahi pū awhe me tētahi pū whawhai ā-ringa e tuku ana i tō pūāhua te painga o te kaha ki te huri i waenganui i ngā huarahi e rua. Tirohia te **Pupuhia ki roto i te Whawhai** me te **Awhe-kore**.

* Ko tētahi pū tuarua kāore i te wā katoa he pū, ka taea he taputapu, ētahi atu huānga whai hua rānei.

  ***Hei tauira,*** *ka whawhai tō pūāhua me tētahi hoari i tētahi ringa me tētahi rama i tērā atu, e whakamahi ana i te rama mō te mārama me tētahi karapu pea. Ka hiahia pea te kaiwhakahaere kēmu i ngā takahuri Waimarie mena ko te mea tuarua he ngāwari, he mōrearea pea (pēnei i te rama hinu).*

Mō tētahi ture o te koromatua, mena he DEX 15, iti iho rānei tō pūāhua, he matau, he mauī rānei rātou. Mena he DEX 16 neke atu rānei tō pūāhua, ka taea e rātou te ambidextrous mena e kōwhiri ana rātou. Mena kāore i whakaritea te ringa-kaha i mua, ā, he āwangawanga, ka tono pea te kaiwhakahaere kēmu ki a koe ki te hanga i tētahi takahuri Waimarie. Ko te angitu motuhake e tuku ana i tō pūāhua ki te ambidextrous. Ko te angitu e tuku ana i a koe ki te kōwhiri, ko te rahua me whakatau me te takahuri D6 (1–3=matau, 4–6=mauī). Kia whakaritea te ringa-kaha, me tuhi e koe ki tō pepa pūāhua mō te tohutoro a muri ake nei.

## Whawhai i raro i te Wai

He iti noa ngā wāhi e hāngai ana te Tangata mō te whawhai, engari i ētahi wā ko te whawhai i te wā e rumakina ana he mea kore e taea te karo. Mena kāore tō pūāhua i te taputapu, i tētahi mana rānei hei aukati i te toremi, tirohia te **Te Taokete, te Toremi, me te Kore Hau**.

He maha ngā pū e kore e hua i raro i te wai, pēnei i ērā e tuku ana i te kino kuru, hinga rānei. Ko ngā pānga o ētahi atu he iti ake i te mea tūturu, ā, ko ētahi (pēnei i ngā pū, ngā pū pūngao rānei) kāore pea e mahi tūturu mena ka rumakina. Ko ngā āhuatanga e whai ake nei e pā ana ki te whawhai i raro i te wai:

* Me angitu tētahi pūāhua kaukau i te takahuri pūkenga Kaukau ia rauna kia neke ki te tūnga kia mahia tētahi whakaeke. Mena kei te tū i runga i te papa, kāore tēnei takahuri e hiahiatia.

* Ko tētahi pū e tuku ana i ētahi atu kino i te kino werohanga e tuku ana i te haurua kino anake (me whakamborotahi ki runga) i te wā e noho ana i raro i te wai.

* Ko te noho i raro i te wai e tatau ana hei whai i te 3 tohu tāpiri o te pūrua ki ētahi atu pū kore-werohanga.

* I te wā e noho ana i raro i te wai, ko ngā whakaeke, ngā parai, me ngā Karo katoa he here ki te pūkenga Kaukau o te kaiwhakaeke, hāunga mena kei roto i te whawhai tata, kei te hopu, me/rānei te whakamahi i tētahi pū werohanga o SIZ 0, 1 rānei.

* Ko te parai me te pākete i te wā e noho ana i raro i te wai he *Hinga*.
* Ko te whawhai i raro i te wai e utu ana i te rua o ngā tohu ngenge tūturu ia rauna whawhai.

I te whakaaro o te kaiwhakahaere kēmu, ko tētahi e noho mārama ana ki te taiao i raro i te wai (merfolk, ētahi kararehe kaukau katoa, hei tauira) kāore e pā ki ētahi o ngā āhuatanga ki runga.

## Pūtea (Vacuum)

Mena ka pā tō pūāhua ki te pūtea me te kore tētahi momo tiaki, ko te mahi tuatahi ko te whakamama i te hau katoa i roto i ō rātou pūkahukahu kia taurite te pēhanga i roto, i waho hoki. Mena kāore tō pūāhua e mahi tēnei, ka tango rātou i te 1D6+2 ngā tohu kino ia rauna whawhai i te hinga o ō rātou pūkahukahu. Mena ka tere te pā ki te pūtea, ko tētahi pūāhua e mōhio ana ki te mahi tēnei ka taea te mahara me tētahi takahuri Whakaaro angitu. Ka taea hoki e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu te tuku i tētahi takahuri *Uaua* Mōhio, i tētahi takahuri Pūtaiao (Ahorangi) kāore i whakarerekē rānei kia tuku i tō pūāhua ki ēnei pārongo.

Tāpiri atu, ko te Tangata e tīmata ana ki te rere toto mena ka pā ki te pūtea, ki ngā āhuatanga pūtea-kore rānei, e tuku ana i te 1 kino ia rauna mō te rauna tuatahi, kātahi ko te 2 kino i te rauna tuarua, 4 kino whai muri i te toru rauna katoa, 8 kino i te rauna tuawhā, me ētahi atu. Ka taea tēnei te whakaroa e te rauna whawhai kotahi ia tohu pūrua o tētahi kākahu tiaki, i te whakaaro o te kaiwhakahaere kēmu.

Tāpiri atu, ko te pā ohorere ki te pūtea ka taea e tō pūāhua te pakaru o ō rātou taringa (hangaia he takahuri Manawanui kia karo i tēnei), ā, whai muri i te CON×2 rauna whawhai i te pūtea, ka tīmata tō rātou kitenga ki te pāngia, e hanga ana i ngā takahuri e whai wāhi ana ki te tirohanga he *Uaua*. I runga i te āhua me te wāhi i pā ai tō pūāhua ki te pūtea, ka pā pea rātou ki ngā pānga o te mātao nui, te wera nui, te irirangi rānei. Tirohia ērā tikanga motuhake mō ētahi atu pārongo. Ko ngā mana me/rānei taputapu te rongoā pai hei whakaiti i tēnei.

## Pupuhitahi (Volley Fire)

Ko ngā ture pupuhitahi e pā ana ki ngā pū maka/a-whaiaro. Ko ēnei he mea whakarewa i te reiti kotahi whakaeke ia rauna whawhai i te nuinga o te wā. Heoi, ka hiahia pea tō pūāhua ki te tuku i ngā whakaeke maha ki tētahi hoariri i te wā poto rawa, hei pū aupēhi, hei karo rānei i tētahi tiaki tino angitu.

Mena ka kōwhiri tō pūāhua ki te maka, ki te pupuhi, ki te whakarewa rānei i ngā pū i te wā e tere ana, whakamahia te whakaurunga whakaeke-ia-rauna o te pū e hāngai ana, me te whakaeke tuatahi i te taumata DEX tūturu me ngā whakaeke e whai ake nei e puta ana i te 5 ngā taumata DEX i muri mai. Ko ngā whakaeke pupuhitahi katoa he *Uaua*. Mena ko ngā taumata DEX mō ētahi whakaeke kei raro i te 0, kāore e taea e tō pūāhua te whakaeke anō. Ahakoa he wātea pea tō pūāhua ki te whakaeke neke atu i ngā whakaeke-ia-rauna, ko tērā nui te here, ā, ko ngā whakaeke taumata DEX katoa kāore i te whakamahia.

***Hei tauira,*** *mena ko tō pūāhua me te DEX 12 e whakamahi ana i te pupuhitahi me ngā kōhatu maka (e whiwhi ana i te rua whakaeke-ia-rauna), ko te maka tuatahi ka puta i te taumata DEX 12, ā, ko te tuarua ka puta i te taumata DEX 7. Ahakoa ko te tupono ki te whakaeke i te taumata DEX 2, ko te kōhatu maka e tuku ana i te rua whakaeke-ia-rauna anake.*

Ko ngā ngoikoretanga ki te pupuhitahi, engari, i tua atu i te noho *Uaua*, ko te whakamahi i te matā i te tere ake, ā, kāore e tuku i te kaiwhakaeke ki te parai, ki te Karo rānei i te wā e whakarewa ana i ngā whakaeke pupuhitahi.

## Te Roa o te Pū

I te whawhai, e toru ngā tawhiti e whakaarohia ana: tata (i roto i te awhe ringa), waenga (1–2 mita te tawhiti), me te roa (2+ mita). I runga i ngā ripanga pū, ko ngā pū whawhai ā-ringa katoa e tuku ana i te whakaurunga mō te SIZ/ Enc o te pū. I te nuinga o te wā, ko te nui o te pū, ko te tūpono o te kaiwhakamahi ki te whiwhi i te pā tuatahi, ki te pupuri rānei i tētahi hoariri e mau ana i tētahi pū poto, ā, aukati i a rātou ki te hanga i ō rātou ake whakaeke. Ko te roa o te pū, engari, ko te matatini ki te whakamahi tūturu i ngā awhe katoa (tirohia te **Whakakapi**).

* Whakamahia ngā taumata DEX tūturu mō te poto vs. poto, poto vs. waenga, waenga vs. waenga, me te roa vs. roa ngā pū.

* Ko tētahi pūāhua e mau ana i te pū roa e whakaeke ana i te tuatahi ki tētahi hoariri e whakamahi ana i te waenga, i te poto rānei, ahakoa tō rātou taumata DEX. Ko te kaiwhakamahi pū-roa e whakaeke ana i tōna taumata DEX mena he teitei ake tērā, i mua noa rānei i te taumata DEX o tōna hoariri (mena he ōrite, he iti iho rānei te DEX i te hoariri).

* Ka taea e tētahi kaiwhakamahi pū-roa te whakamātau ki te pupuri i tētahi kaiwhakamahi pū-poto, me hanga e rātou he parai angitu, he Karo rānei kia karo i tōna kaitiaki. Kia paheke te kaiwhakamahi pū-poto ki roto i te kaitiaki o te kaiwhakamahi pū-roa, ko ngā whakaeke ki te kaiwhakamahi pū-roa he Māmā, ko ngā whakaeke ki te kaiwhakamahi pū-poto he *Uaua*. Ko ngā parai a te kaiwhakamahi pū-roa he *Uaua*.
* Kia whakahoki i te awhe whawhai tino pai, me tuu i tētahi kaiwhakaeke pea ki te kino anō, ka taea e tētahi kaiwhakamahi pū-roa te Karo ki te wehe, ki te hoki ki te tawhiti e taea e rātou te pupuri i te kaiwhakaeke i te taha anō, ki te maka rānei i tō rātou pū, ki te Whawhai, Hopu, hinga me tētahi pākete, ki te kukume me te whakaeke me tētahi pū waenga, poto rānei.

* Ko te āhuatanga i whakaaturia ki runga kāore i te pono mō te waenga vs. poto ngā pū, kāore i whakarerekēhia, ā, kāore e hiahiatia ngā tikanga motuhake kia whakakapi ki te whawhai.

Ko ētahi pū, pēnei i ngā quarterstaffs, he tino urutau, ā, ka taea te whakamahi ki te whakaeke i ētahi o ngā tawhiti e toru.

## Tikanga Huarere

Ko ngā tikanga huarere e kapi ana i ngā pānga ā-tūmatanui katoa e pā ana ki ngā pūāhua: te matangi, te kapua, me te ua. Ko ngā pānga mai i ētahi atu pito o te huarere e kapi ana i te **Mātao, Whakaaturanga, Hiakai, me te Hiamoe** me te **Mātao me te Wera**.

### Matangi

Ko ngā matangi kaha ka pā ki te nekehanga me te pū pere (ngā pū maka me te pū pere, kāore i te pū, i te pū pūngao rānei), me ētahi atu mahi e pai ake ai te hau mārie. Ko te neke i runga i te wae, i te eke rānei e pāngia ana e te matangi (tirohia ngā **Whakarerekē Whenua me te Huarere**). Tirohia te momo matangi ki raro.

Ko te uara STR e whakaritea ana e te kaiwhakahaere kēmu, mō ngā takahuri ātete STR vs. STR ia rauna kia haere tonu tō pūāhua ki te tū tūturu, ā, ko te whakarerekē e tukuna ana ki ngā pūkenga e hāngai ana (ngā pū pere, ngā pūkenga neke, me ētahi atu) ā, ka taea te whakamahi hei whakarerekē i te reiti MOV. Ko ēnei uara e whakaatu ana i te whakapā tūturu ki te kaha katoa o te matangi, ā, ka iti ake pea i te tawhiti atu i te pokapū, i runga rānei i te uhi kapi.

* **Matangi Māmā ki te Waenga (STR 0–1):** —

* **Matangi Kaha (STR 2–6):** –10%

* **Matangi Tino Kaha (STR 7–9):** –25%

* **Āwhā (STR 10–12):** –50%

* **Matangi Ruarīra (STR 13–15):** –75%

* **Matangi Tāwhiri (STR 16+):** –90%

Ko te rahua mō te takahuri ātete te tikanga ka hinga tō pūāhua; ka tango pea i te kino (tirohia te **Hinga**).

### Kapua Kapi

Ko te kapua kapi e tautuhi ana i te maha o te rangi e kapi ana i ngā kapua. Ko te whakarerekē e whakatau ana hoki i te tupono ōrau o te ua (takahurihia te whiu hei tupono, kaua hei whakarerekē, tirohia te **Ua**, ki raro). Ko ngā pūkenga e pāngia ana e te kapua kapi ko te Whakatere me te Mōhio (Ahorangi). Ka whakarerekē pea tēnei i ngā mana, i ētahi āhuatanga rānei e hiahiatia ana te rā tūturu. Ko te kapua kapi nui, katoa rānei ka noho pea hei pōuri kapi (tirohia te **Pōuri**).

* **Kapua Kapi Māmā ki te Waenga:** —

* **Kapua Kapi Nui:** –25%

* **Kapua Kapi Tino Kaha:** –50%

* **Kapua Kapi Katoa:** –90%

### Ua

Mō ngā rā me ngā tikanga huarere e tika ana, mena e hiahiatia ana, takahurihia te D100 ia rā. Mena ko te otinga he ōrite ki te iti iho rānei i te ōrau o te rangi i kapi (tirohia te **Kapua Kapi**, ki runga), ka ua. Ko te nui o te ua e hinga ana e ōrite ana ki te takahuri D100, i roto i ngā mita. I runga i te huarere me te wā o te tau, ka huri pea te ua ki te huka, te ua hukapapa, te kōhatu rānei. Ko te whakarerekē e pā ana ki ngā pūkenga e hāngai ana katoa.

* **Ua:** –10%

* **Huka:** –25%

* **Ua Hukapapa:** –50%

* **Kōhatu:** –75%

## Whawhai i te Kore-kiriata (Zero-gravity)

Mena kei te ātea tō pūāhua, kei tētahi taiao rānei me te iti, te kore rānei o te kiriata, me hanga e rātou he takahuri Kaha ia rauna e whakamātau ana rātou ki te mahi i ētahi mahi whawhai, Tinana rānei. Ko te angitu e tohu ana ka taea e tō pūāhua te mahi i ngā mahi kāore i whakarerekē, ko te rahua i tēnei takahuri te tikanga ko ngā mahi katoa he *Uaua*.

Tāpiri atu, hāunga mena kei te whakapakari tō pūāhua, ko tētahi pānga ka pāngia e rātou ka puta te pānga motuhake hinga me te haurua o te pānga motuhake hinga ki te kaiwhakaeke hoki, e tika ana (tirohia te **Hinganga**). Heoi, ko te kino mai i te pā ki ētahi atu mea e whakaititia ana ki te haurua (me whakamborotahi ki runga).

Mena he huarahi e whiriwhiri ai rātou i te taiao (hu kiriata, me ētahi atu), ko te takahuri Kaha e tuku ana i a rātou kia whakahāngaitia e hiahia ana. Ko te rahua e tohu ana i a rātou ki te huarahi hē, e anga ana pea ki waho i tētahi hoariri, ki runga-i-raro rānei e tika ana ki tō rātou whakahāngaitanga e hiahiatia ana.
